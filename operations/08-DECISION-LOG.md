# LORE — Decision Log

This file records decisions that are considered locked unless explicitly revisited.

## 2026-09-11

### Brand name

**LORE**

### Master tagline

**COLLECT THE INTERNET.**

### Brand thought

**ICONS ARE MADE OF MOMENTS.**

### Six-card collection line

**SIX MOMENTS. ONE ICON.**

### Product direction

LORE launches as a premium physical/digital collectible concept first.

A card game is **not** required for v1.

### Creator strategy

Initial focus: internet creators/influencers who can approve their likeness, participate in the collection and potentially promote it to their communities.

### Moment strategy

Each creator's six cards must be based on six researched, sourced, approved moments from their public history.

### Rarity ladder

Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythic

### Art direction

Premium modern shōnen/manga energy with bold ink, expressive characters, cel-shaded colour and cinematic storytelling.

Do not directly imitate a specific anime franchise or artist.

### Logo direction

Brush-lettered LORE wordmark + simple crown mark.

Core palette: Obsidian / Bone / Gold.

### QR architecture

Visible public QR points to a LORE-owned moment page/redirect.

Physical ownership uses a separate concealed one-time claim credential.

### Complete-set physical product

Official six-slot display frame for a creator's full collection.

Display line: **SIX MOMENTS. ONE ICON.**

### Digital product

Collector Vault + creator collection pages + population tracking + serial tracking + social sharing + creator partner dashboard.

### Crypto / NFT

Not part of the initial product direction.
