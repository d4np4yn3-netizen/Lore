# LORE — option 04 approval and release

**Revision: LORE-04-v1.0. Approved by Daniel Payne on 11 September 2026.**

Daniel selected **04 / Rising Strokes** from the four-crown comparison after clarifying that the earlier crown was too close to the L. The selected proof increases separation. This exact crown, its placement and the existing LORE wordmark are now locked.

His instruction was: “Yup, let’s lock 4 in, update the github, remove the old files so there is no confusion, create the required PNG and vector files, and do an updated branding page let’s go!”

This authorises promotion of the selected master, deterministic PNG/SVG derivatives, the updated brand page, and removal of superseded repository assets. It supersedes the earlier flat geometric crown lock and the export hold. It is not approval to redesign the selected mark again.

The pack contains 28 designs, each in PNG and SVG: primary and compact logos, horizontal lockups, standalone crown and wordmark, the three locked brand lines, social avatars and an app icon. Horizontal lockups and standalone/social crops are deterministic compositions of the same approved geometry.
