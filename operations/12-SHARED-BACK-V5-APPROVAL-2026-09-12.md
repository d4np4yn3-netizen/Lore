# Shared back v5 approval — 12 September 2026

## Explicit decision

Daniel requested removal of the diamond, a thicker outer gold border and a more visible thin inner border. The resulting `LORE-Shared-Back-v5.png` was presented in full, with the corresponding native SVG retained.

Daniel then approved that exact revision: “Ok lock that in and update the github, then we will decide what’s next”.

## Approved scope

- Lock **LORE-BACK-v5**, using the exact reviewed PNG and SVG bytes.
- Remove the diamond; use an outer gold stroke of 14 and an inner stroke of 2 at 60% opacity on the 900 × 1260 canvas.
- Preserve the exact approved crown, wordmark, tagline, gold rule and all their positions.
- Use the same back across creators and rarities.
- Combine the back approval with the unchanged selected **LORE-FRONT-v3** layout and illustration reference to lock the visual card system as **LORE-CARD-v1.0**.
- Update GitHub's current assets and standards so the superseded diamond back cannot be mistaken for the current master.

The v3 back assets are removed from the active tree; its former review page redirects to the approved v5 master. Git history retains the earlier proposal. The v4 comparison was review-only and is not an approved asset.

## Scope that remains open

This is design approval, not a manufacturing proof. The previous response explicitly reserved final cutting margins for the printer's template. Physical dimensions, bleed, cutting tolerance, stock, print/foil colour and physical QR scanning remain pending. Creator rights, final moment selection, live QR routes and concealed claiming are not approved by this decision.
