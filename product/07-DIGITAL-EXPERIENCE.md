# LORE — Digital Experience

## Core digital proposition

The website/app extends the physical collection. It should make collecting, completing, discovering and showing off feel satisfying without requiring a game.

## Collector Vault

Each collector account should have a personal Vault showing:

- total cards claimed
- total unique cards
- overall set completion
- number of complete six-card creator collections
- Mythics, Legendaries and other rarity counts
- serial-numbered cards
- collection rank / badges where appropriate
- privacy controls for valuable cards

## Creator collection page

Each creator should have a six-card digital display with:

- 0/6 to 6/6 completion
- card artwork
- rarity
- moment title
- source story
- original source link
- total population / claimed population where appropriate
- numbered-card tracker where appropriate

On completion, celebrate:

**ICON COMPLETE**

**SIX MOMENTS. ONE ICON.**

## Population tracking

Collectors should be able to see how much of a limited population has been claimed.

Example:

- Mythic population: 25
- Claimed: 19
- Unclaimed: 6

Do not publicly expose a collector's identity or valuable holdings without explicit opt-in.

## Shareable collection content

LORE should generate attractive social-ready cards for:

- Mythic pull/claim
- six-card creator completion
- collector Vault milestones
- serial-numbered discoveries
- creator leaderboard movement

The output should be designed primarily for X/social sharing but remain platform-agnostic.

## Creator partner dashboard

Approved creators should see:

- collector count
- total cards claimed
- complete six-card sets
- rarity discovery counts
- traffic to their original source moments
- referral traffic / attributable sales where applicable
- creator rank / growth where appropriate

## Creator competition

Leaderboards may create friendly competition between participating creators.

Examples:

- most collected creator
- fastest-growing creator collection
- most completed six-card set

These should be framed as fun community competition, not as a definitive ranking of a person's real-world importance.

## X integration

Potential integrations:

- sign in/connect with X using approved mechanisms
- optional display of X handle
- one-click social share assets
- creator tagging where appropriate
- referral links such as creator-specific collection pages

Do not depend on scraping or unofficial platform access.

## Marketplace

A secondary marketplace is not required for v1.

LORE should first prove:

1. people want the physical cards
2. they enjoy claiming and displaying them digitally
3. creators promote and participate

Marketplace design can follow later if demand justifies it.
