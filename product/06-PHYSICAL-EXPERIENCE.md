# LORE — Physical Experience

## Product philosophy

LORE should feel desirable before a buyer understands every feature.

The physical card, finish, packaging and display must create the reaction:

> I want to own that.

## Core physical products

### Trading cards

Premium collectible cards across six rarity tiers.

### Booster / drop packaging

Packaging should feel premium, dark, graphic and immediately recognisable as LORE.

### Six-card creator display

Each creator's full six-card collection should fit into an official horizontal six-slot display.

The display exists to turn completion into a physical achievement.

Required direction:

- six equal card positions
- clean premium border
- creator nameplate
- LORE crown/wordmark treatment
- line: `SIX MOMENTS. ONE ICON.`
- desk or wall presentation considered
- protect cards from routine handling
- explore magnetic/recessed card mounting
- investigate UV-resistant materials for premium versions

## Display tiers to investigate

### Collector Frame

Accessible display product for completed six-card collections.

### Icon Vault

Premium display version using upgraded materials, engraved plaque, stronger protection and potentially subtle lighting.

Pricing is not locked and must follow manufacturing quotes.

## Set presentation

When all six cards are displayed together, the collection should feel like a visual biography.

The cards may contain subtle connecting composition, colour or narrative progression, but they must not depend on the frame to work individually.

## Packaging identity

Core packaging should normally use:

- Obsidian base
- Bone/white LORE brush wordmark
- Gold crown/accent
- creator art as the main source of colour

Avoid over-designing packaging with every rarity colour at once.

## Prototype requirement

Before committing to production quantities, manufacture at least one complete creator prototype containing all six rarity finishes.

Evaluate in hand:

- print sharpness
- black levels
- colour saturation
- foil registration
- reflectivity
- surface scratching
- card curl
- edge quality
- perceived weight and quality
- display-frame fit

Digital mockups are not sufficient to lock physical finishes.
