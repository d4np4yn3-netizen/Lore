# LORE Crypto Booster Pack v1 - Review

This is the first packaging review for **CRYPTO • SEASON 01**.

## Source of truth
- Exact approved LORE logo files from `brand/assets/png/`.
- Approved crypto artwork only, pulled from the current visually-approved Season 01 card masters.
- No card artwork is regenerated.

## Printer geometry
Built to the QPMN booster template supplied by Dan:
- Full bleed: **6.77 × 5.19 in**
- Final output: **2031 × 1557 px @ 300 DPI**
- Cut area: approximately **6.54 × 4.94 in**
- Vertical folds are derived from the supplied PDF template.
- Heat-seal areas are kept free of critical type.

The printer template itself says to extend artwork through the bleed/heat-seal regions and hide the template before exporting PNG/JPEG at 300 DPI.
