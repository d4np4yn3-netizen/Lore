# 066 - Bored Ape Yacht Club

23 APR 2021 / NFT CULTURE / UNCOMMON

WELCOME TO THE CLUB

## The story

On 23 April 2021, Bored Ape Yacht Club began the release of its 10,000-piece NFT collection. BAYC's own founding history marks that date as the arrival of the Bored Apes. The project paired a set of distinct digital characters with the idea of belonging to a shared club.

The founders' retrospective makes the launch sequence clearer. A week-long presale began on 23 April at 0.08 ETH per ape. Only about 500 were minted during that first week; the collection sold out on 1 May. The card therefore marks the start of the presale, rather than the later sellout. Network gas fees were separate from the mint price.

Membership was central to the premise. Yuga describes each NFT as a pass to a digital club, with identity and community built around the characters. The founders imagined crypto traders who had become wealthy and were now bored, gathering at a secret clubhouse in a swamp. That fictional setting gave the collection its deliberately unglamorous sense of humour.

This illustration turns that idea into a humid evening at a wooden doorway. Three original, fictional apes occupy the porch, and one presents a portrait card to a glowing reader. The scene is an interpretation of membership culture: it depicts neither a documented launch-day gathering nor specific official Bored Ape tokens.

The tag rack and mint-price sign carry the launch's numerical clues. A small postcard reproduces the approved pixel portrait from HISTROVE card 038, linking this scene to the earlier CryptoPunks chapter. WELCOME TO THE CLUB is approved editorial wording for this card, not a quotation from the founders or a historical launch announcement.

## Details in the artwork

1. **THE TEN THOUSAND TAGS** The 10,000 sign above the hanging tags points to the collection's fixed supply. The small rack is an illustrative shorthand for that total, not a literal inventory or a documented launch-day object.
2. **THE MINT PRICE** MINT 0.08 ETH records the original price to mint an ape. Ethereum transaction gas was an additional cost. The sign refers to the initial mint, not a later resale value.
3. **THE MEMBERSHIP READER** The portrait card and green reader translate NFT membership into a physical doorway. Both props are invented symbols; the illustration does not claim that BAYC used this hardware or issued these plastic cards.
4. **THE PIXEL PORTRAIT POSTCARD** The postcard uses an actual crop of approved HISTROVE 038 artwork, including its small 10,000 caption. Perspective-fitted inside the original paper border, it is a deliberate cross-card reference to CryptoPunks.
