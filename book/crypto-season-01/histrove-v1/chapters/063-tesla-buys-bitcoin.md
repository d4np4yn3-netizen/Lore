# 063 - Tesla Buys Bitcoin

08 FEB 2021 / TESLA / UNCOMMON

BITCOIN ON THE BALANCE SHEET.

## The story

On 8 February 2021, Tesla filed its 2020 annual report and disclosed an aggregate $1.50 billion investment in bitcoin. The card marks that public disclosure. It does not place the purchases on the filing date or claim a single transaction.

Tesla had changed its investment policy in January 2021, allowing some cash beyond operating needs to enter alternative reserve assets. The bitcoin investment followed that change. January is the policy date in the filing; the precise purchase day is not given.

The Investments disclosure appears in Note 23, Subsequent Events: a development after the 2020 year end. Bitcoin payments were still described as a future plan, subject to applicable laws and initially limited. They were not announced as already available on this date.

In the illustration, the recurring professor reads the filing at a desk inside a night-lit showroom. A warm lamp draws attention to the highlighted asset and amount, while the silver car stays in the background. The goggles, swept-back white hair and familiar face connect him to card 047, carrying a fictional observer into this new scene.

Beside the paperwork, an envelope marked FUTURE holds another visual thread: a small car silhouette recalls card 059. The January tab, Note 23 marker and February receipt invite the reader to distinguish policy, disclosure and imagination. BITCOIN ON THE BALANCE SHEET frames the treasury story; the document, room and character are an illustrated interpretation.

## Details in the artwork

1. **THE JANUARY TAB** JAN 2021 marks the investment-policy change. It does not establish a purchase day. The tab sits above the report, separating that timing from the February receipt.
2. **BITCOIN AND $1.50B** The highlighted pair names the asset and aggregate investment. Their yellow strips form the scene's focal point; the surrounding page lines are illustrative.
3. **NOTE 23 AND THE FILING RECEIPT** NOTE 23 points to Subsequent Events. The separate receipt carries the filing date, 08 FEB 2021. The paper design is invented, not an authentic SEC facsimile.
4. **THE RETURNING PROFESSOR** White hair, goggles and a lab coat bring back the fictional professor from card 047. He acts as the collection's observer, not a real Tesla employee or historical witness.
5. **THE FUTURE ENVELOPE** A car silhouette on the FUTURE envelope echoes card 059. This cross-card clue sits beside the filing, linking the collection's visual stories without making a historical claim.
