# 100 - New Sheriff in Town

18 JUL 2025 / GENIUS ACT / RARE

NEW RULES. SAME FRONTIER.

## The story

At dusk, a sheriff stands beside a fresh notice on a timber wall. An older wanted poster still peeks out above it.

On 18 July 2025, President Donald Trump signed the GENIUS Act into law. Its subject was payment stablecoins: digital assets designed for payment or settlement, with an issuer's obligation to redeem them for a fixed monetary value. The law established a framework for their issuance and supervision, including federal and qualifying state regulatory paths.

For permitted issuers, the Act sets requirements for at least one-to-one backing with specified liquid reserves, public redemption policies and monthly disclosures of reserve composition. Behind the familiar promise of a stable value, Congress set out concrete obligations about the assets held and the information customers could see.

Signing began a new chapter rather than completing the rollout. Rulemaking and the Act's effective-date provisions left implementation work ahead, and payment stablecoins were not made government-guaranteed money. The notice on this wall names a specific part of the market, not every token in town.

The hundredth card leaves the street open beyond the porch. A new rulebook has arrived; the frontier continues.

## Details in the artwork

1. **THE SHERIFF'S STAR** A plain gold star catches the sunset against the navy coat. The new sheriff gives regulatory oversight a familiar frontier face.
2. **THE NOTICE ON THE WALL** GENIUS ACT, PAYMENT STABLECOINS and 18 JUL 2025 keep the event, statutory subject and signing date visible together.
3. **THE OLDER WANTED POSTER** The Cryptoqueen from 044 reappears on the WANTED poster behind the new notice. This collection callback brings an earlier unresolved story onto the final card's wall.
4. **THE OPEN FRONTIER** The timber porch opens onto a street that continues towards the distant hills, leaving the frontier visible beyond the sheriff and the new notice.
