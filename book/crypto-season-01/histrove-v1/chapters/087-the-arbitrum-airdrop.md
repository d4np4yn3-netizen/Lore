# 087 - The Arbitrum Airdrop

23 MARCH 2023 / ARBITRUM / COMMON

CLAIMED.

## The story

On 23 March 2023, eligible users could begin claiming ARB, the governance token associated with the Arbitrum DAO. Eligibility reflected activity recorded before a 6 February snapshot. This was a token-distribution and governance milestone, not the launch of the network: Arbitrum had already been operating on mainnet for nearly eighteen months.

Allocations varied with qualifying activity, including different weighting before and after the Nitro upgrade and checks intended to limit Sybil farming. Individual allocations ranged from 625 to 10,250 ARB. The 1,250 ARB on this screen is one possible allocation, not a universal reward. Receiving tokens opened a route to participation in governance.

The imagined coinburst turns an on-screen claim into a moment of celebration. Its calendar and snapshot date anchor that fantasy to the historical event, while the Arbitrum One label identifies the network. The ETH fee line makes another distinction: ARB was the governance token, while transactions on Arbitrum One used ETH for gas. The displayed fee is illustrative, not a verified historical charge.

## Details in the artwork

1. **23 MAR calendar** The desk calendar marks 23 March 2023, when eligible users could begin claiming ARB.
2. **06 FEB snapshot** The screen points to the 6 February snapshot used to establish airdrop eligibility.
3. **ARBITRUM ONE** The network label locates the imagined claim on Arbitrum One, rather than depicting a new network launch.
4. **ETH gas fee** The ETH symbol distinguishes transaction gas from ARB governance tokens. The 0.0012 ETH readout is illustrative.
