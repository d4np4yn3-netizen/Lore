# 077 - Crypto for Ukraine

03 MAR 2022 / UKRAINE / COMMON

IN SUPPORT OF UKRAINE

## The story

After Russia invaded Ukraine, cryptocurrency donations became part of the international response. The government quickly established official channels through which supporters could contribute.

A Ministry of Digital Transformation update published on 4 March 2022 reported that more than $53 million in cryptocurrency had been raised as of 3 March. The card marks that reported milestone, not the invasion date or a final fundraising total.

The announcement listed Bitcoin, Ethereum, Tether, Polkadot and Dogecoin among the official donation options. The artwork’s dispatch monitor references two of those channels.

The government said the vast majority of the funds supported the Ukrainian army; the remainder went to humanitarian initiatives and broader support. The pictured relief supplies represent one part of that response, not the allocation of the whole total.

This imagined warehouse scene connects incoming contributions with practical coordination: a volunteer checks deliveries while a terminal records donations. The setting and its props are storytelling devices, not a reconstruction of a documented shipment.

## Details in the artwork

1. **THE DONATION TERMINAL** Bitcoin and Ethereum symbols reference two officially listed donation channels. The screen and workstation are illustrative.
2. **THE DATED REPORT** 03 MAR 2022 and $53M+ mark the reported fundraising snapshot. The folder is invented; it does not price the supplies.
3. **BLUE AND YELLOW TAPE** Packing tape carries Ukraine’s colours across neighboring cartons: a symbolic connection between solidarity and practical support.
4. **THE PINEAPPLE ENVELOPE** A pineapple stamp and blank sender line recall card 050, The Pineapple Fund. This crossover does not identify an actual donor.
