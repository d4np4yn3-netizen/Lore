## Proposed two-page entry pattern

Page one gives the approved card front room to breathe alongside its number, rarity, year and phrase. Page two pairs the historical explanation with four decoded art details and a primary-source/art note. The PDF is an A4 editorial layout study so the text can be reviewed now; final book trim, binding, typography, colour reproduction, rights and printer specifications are not locked. The build script for 001 is [build_001_spread.py](build_001_spread.py). Repeat this pattern only after its wording and reading layout are approved.
