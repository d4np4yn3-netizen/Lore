# LORE

**COLLECT THE INTERNET.**

[![LORE — approved Rising Strokes identity](brand/previews/LORE-Brand-Guide.png)](brand/README.md)

**Brand locked: 04 / Rising Strokes.** [Branding page](brand/README.md) · [PNG + SVG pack](brand/downloads/LORE-Brand-Pack-v1.0.zip) · [Individual assets](brand/assets/README.md)

LORE is a premium internet-culture collectible brand that turns defining moments from internet culture into physical and digital collectible cards.

## Locked brand language

- **Master tagline:** COLLECT THE INTERNET.
- **Brand thought:** ICONS ARE MADE OF MOMENTS.
- **Six-card collection line:** SIX MOMENTS. ONE ICON.

## Core product idea

**Season One: Crypto. Season Two: Gaming Creators (planned).**
[Crypto rules and current cards](cards/crypto/README.md) include the approved
[Pizza Day Legendary](cards/crypto/season-01/pizza-day-master-01/README.md).
Crypto assigns one rarity per event, with standard and foil finishes at every
tier. Event dates replace creator moment counters. Set size and quantities
remain undecided. This extension preserves LORE's existing layout and anime style.

Each featured creator receives a six-card collection built from six genuine, researched moments from their public history. Each card reimagines one real moment as premium anime/manga-inspired collectible art.

The six rarity tiers are:

1. Common
2. Uncommon
3. Rare
4. Epic
5. Legendary
6. Mythic

Rarity changes scarcity and collectability; crypto foil is a separate finish available at every tier — not the importance or factual significance of the underlying moment.

## Product ecosystem

- Physical collectible cards
- Secure digital claiming
- Collector Vault
- Creator profile and partner dashboards
- Card population and serial tracking
- Creator and collector leaderboards
- Shareable social collection moments
- Official six-card creator display frames
- QR links back to the original post, video, stream or source moment
