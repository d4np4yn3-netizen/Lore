# LORE Brand Visual Reference

## Approved direction

The approved LORE identity is the **01 / The Mark** direction: custom aggressive brush-script `LORE` wordmark with a compact crown above it.

### Core rules

1. The logo must remain highly legible at card-corner, pack, website-header and social-avatar sizes.
2. Primary form: white/black brush `LORE` lettering plus restrained metallic-gold crown.
3. Do not turn the master logo into a rarity-coloured rainbow logo; rarity colour belongs to the cards.
4. Anime/manga energy belongs mainly in surrounding artwork and textures, not in excessive ornament on the logo itself.
5. The crown must work independently as the micro/app/social mark.
6. The brush silhouette of `LORE` must eventually be redrawn as a custom production vector wordmark rather than relying permanently on generated lettering.
7. The earlier JPEG is not an approved master and must not be used for production.
