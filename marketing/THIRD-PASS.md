## How to use the new coverage

Start with the explicit product/seller invitations and clearly relevant shops, then culture/education partners. Before broader-crypto packs are proposed to Bitcoin-only audiences, disclose the complete scope and check acceptance. Do not promise an unapproved Bitcoin-only edition. Foreign-language materials, rights, sample quality, landed costs, returns, price, margin, MOQ and inventory remain to be established.

Paid media routes added for French, German and Japanese audiences are channels for later evaluation, not customers buying inventory. Request current rates and transparent sponsored labels; no independent editorial endorsement or audience conversion is promised. Use a single coordinated approach per account group.
