# HISTROVE outreach readiness

Research snapshot: 8 October 2026. This is a preparation checklist, not legal advice or permission to contact anybody. Public contact information is not consent to marketing, proof of buyer interest, or endorsement of HISTROVE.

## Product and offer

- Confirm the final 100-card collection, 10-card pack contents, print/foil specification, packaging, book format and language
- Approve physical samples, handling quality, camera QR tests, print resolution and manufacturing tolerances before describing stock as production-ready
- Establish retail and wholesale pricing, currencies, tax treatment, minimum order quantities, case quantities, wholesale margin, payment terms and returns
- Establish actual stock, production lead time, fulfilment origin, shipping markets and delivery estimates. A target to take orders in December 2026 is not a guaranteed shipping date
- Complete commercial-use review of artwork, trademarks, likenesses, historical quotations and third-party logos; no prospect is a partner without its consent
- Choose a clearly described pilot: retail trial, event merchandise, corporate gift, editorial review or paid creator collaboration. Do not combine all five in a generic pitch
- Avoid investment, token-redemption, guaranteed rarity-value or profit claims. The proposition is a physical history collectible and companion book

## Sender and destination

- Confirm the public brand domain, live product/preview page, accurate sender identity and monitored reply mailbox
- Set up and test SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for the chosen sending domain; use normal provider-supported TLS and unsubscribe handling. Google recommends all three authentication methods; exact mandatory requirements differ by sender volume ([Gmail sender guidelines](https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126?hl=en))
- Reopen the recorded contact source immediately before sending; confirm that the destination still belongs to the intended company/creator and accepts the proposed kind of enquiry
- A form that sells a company's services is not automatically a merchandise-buying channel. Prefer an explicit partnership/buying route; otherwise ask for the appropriate team without pretending to be a customer or existing partner
- Do not use security, vulnerability, legal, privacy, complaints, careers or press-only inboxes for sales pitches. Do not invent an order number to get through support
- Confirm recipient jurisdiction and legal entity type. UK rules distinguish corporate subscribers from sole traders and some partnerships; processing named business contacts may also engage UK GDPR. Other jurisdictions have their own rules ([ICO B2B guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/))
- Establish applicable consent/lawful-basis and transparency requirements, sender/contact disclosures, simple opt-out and a suppression list before a campaign. Honour objections and do not add researched contacts to newsletters

## Creator campaigns

- Verify the creator's current official business channel, representative and audience fit before any proposal
- Request recent audience geography, typical views, engagement quality, rate card, deliverables and conflicts only through the business channel; no follower figures or campaign prices are inferred here
- Agree fair compensation or gifted-sample terms, editorial independence, usage rights, approval boundaries, tracking and disclosure obligations before a collaboration
- Paid, gifted and affiliate connections need clear disclosure where applicable; a free product can create a material connection. Platform labels alone may be insufficient ([FTC influencer guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers))
- Avoid making an independent review contingent on praise. No financial advice or return claims should be attached to the cards

## Event proposals

- Reconfirm date, venue, application deadline, merchandise/retail policy, available allocation and audience before budgeting
- Distinguish supplying an organiser from paying to sponsor/exhibit. An exhibitor form is a route to a conversation, not a purchase order
- Check exclusivity, shipping/customs, insurance, sample requirements, cancellation terms, minimum runs and unsold-stock responsibility
- Near-term 2026 events may be useful introductions only; do not promise manufactured delivery before the product is ready

## Small pilot, then learn

Select a small approved group, personalise the angle, and obtain send approval. Keep private correspondence, quotes, sample addresses, opt-outs and relationship notes in a private system, not this public repository. Track qualified replies, buyer referrals, sample requests and viable commercial conversations rather than treating a large address list as success.
