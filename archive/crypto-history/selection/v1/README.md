Card moments receive featured entries with exact approved artwork as it becomes available. Supporting events use compact entries. The prior inline example is a layout direction, not a final branded site approval. Build with the existing approved LORE assets, fonts, colours and wording. This milestone does not deploy or modify the website.

## Selection and research policy

This pass uses the saved year shards, not the monolithic discovery archive. It manually curates for historical significance, a clear story, visual potential and coverage across eras. No arbitrary numerical score or equal per-year quota was imposed.

The existing discovery archive remains available for later additions. Further research now targets the chosen events and specific coverage gaps; completing all 1,229 discovery candidates is no longer a prerequisite for this review selection. Two gaps were filled with primary-sourced 2025 records: the official TRUMP token announcement and the Ross Ulbricht pardon.

The chronology spans 1976–26 September 2026. The proposed cards stop at 2025; 2026 appears as supporting context with provisional historical perspective. This avoids committing current-year developments to cards before their significance has settled. This boundary is an editorial recommendation for review.

Keep source-established date precision. A month or year is not silently converted into a specific day. Distinguish announcement, launch, implementation and later reporting. In particular, preserve the approved BitConnect **2017 presentation** separately from the **2018 collapse**, and the approved Buried Fortune year from its later publicity date. Timeline overrides are explicit in the structured data and do not silently rewrite year research.
