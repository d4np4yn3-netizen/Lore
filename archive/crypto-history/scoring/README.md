# LORE Crypto Archive — Card Scoring Layer

This folder evaluates researched historical events for possible physical LORE cards.

It does **not** alter the historical evidence. History remains in `../years/`.

## Eligibility gate

An event may be independently scored only when:

- it is a canonical evidence record, not a duplicate/context-only row;
- it has at least one supporting source;
- it is not marked future / not-yet-history;
- unresolved `NEEDS_SOURCE`, `RESEARCH_NEEDED`, claim-rewrite or equivalent records are excluded until resolved;
- subevents linked to a canonical event normally support that event rather than compete against it.

## 100-point scoring model

| Dimension | Max | Question |
|---|---:|---|
| Historical importance | 25 | Did this materially change crypto's development, adoption, regulation, technology or market structure? |
| Story / LORE strength | 20 | Is the human story memorable, strange, dramatic, funny, infamous or emotionally strong? |
| Visual card potential | 15 | Can the moment become a distinctive, readable, premium illustration? |
| Cultural recognition | 15 | Is the moment/project/person recognizable inside crypto or wider internet culture? |
| Uniqueness / timeline value | 10 | Does it add something genuinely different rather than repeat another stronger card? |
| Collectability / chase appeal | 10 | Would collectors actively want this moment even without knowing its score? |
| Evidence confidence | 5 | How strong and precise are the sources/date/claim? |

**Maximum: 100.**

## Editorial bands

- **90–100 — FLAGSHIP**: extremely strong card candidate.
- **80–89 — STRONG**: should receive serious collection consideration.
- **70–79 — VIABLE**: good candidate; selection depends on balance and competition.
- **60–69 — BUBBLE**: useful if it fills an era/category gap.
- **Below 60 — ARCHIVE/BOOK**: normally better as timeline, book or supporting LORE.

These bands are **not physical rarity tiers**.

## Selection rules

Raw score alone never determines the final collection. Final selection must also consider:

- chronological coverage;
- Bitcoin / Ethereum / alt-L1 / DeFi / NFT / stablecoin / exchange / hack / government / people / culture balance;
- avoiding multiple cards that tell essentially the same story;
- season structure;
- existing approved LORE artwork;
- whether related subevents should collapse into a single stronger card.

## Physical rarity

Card rarity (Common / Uncommon / Rare / Epic / Legendary / Mythic) is assigned **after selection** and remains separate from historical importance.
