# Next proposed card — First Transfer

Assistant recommendation, 19 September 2026. Concept only; no new visual approval.
Subject BITCOIN; proposed Legendary; event date 12 JAN 2009 (block date in UTC).
Proposed title FIRST / TRANSFER. Proposed phrase I WAS THE RECIPIENT.

## Art direction

A vast rain-dark city of unlit relay towers. For the first time, one warm golden
signal bridges the gap between two distant stations. Hal Finney is prominent at
the receiving balcony, face lit by the arriving signal, one hand beside a modest
early-computer receiver. A restrained smile of discovery rather than a superhero
pose. The signal arcs behind him through the city; only the two endpoints and
connecting path illuminate. Wet rooftop surfaces, deep blue/teal atmosphere,
confident anime ink, cel shadows and powerful golden light. The scene should feel
like the instant a new connection becomes possible.

The towers, rain, clothes, balcony and physical receiver are symbolic inventions,
not a reconstruction of Hal's home or a claim about the network's geography.
Use a verified public photograph for Hal's likeness before generation, alongside
the original approved LORE style references. Do not invent a face, nationality,
location or silhouette for Satoshi: the sending station has no visible operator.
Keep Hal's face and the signal above the card title and clear of the header.

## Four small in-world Easter eggs

1. 170 engraved on a junction box: the block containing the transfer.
2. 10 BTC on the receiver's small readout: the payment Hal described receiving.
3. RPOW on a worn notebook or pin: Hal's earlier proof-of-work currency experiment,
   which he discusses in the same personal account.
4. v0.1 on the terminal's startup strip: the first publicly released Bitcoin software.

All props are symbolic; the terminal is not claimed to be an exact historical UI.
Do not add a modern Bitcoin logo to a purported 2009 software facsimile. No printed
coin count is necessary; the small verified numerical display carries the amount.

## Primary and ledger evidence

- https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=155054.0
  Hal Finney's own Bitcoin and me account, published 19 March 2013. In the paragraph
  beginning When Satoshi announced the first release, he identifies himself as the
  recipient of the first bitcoin transaction and says Satoshi sent ten coins as a
  test. I WAS THE RECIPIENT. is an exact continuous excerpt, with normalised capitals
  and punctuation. It is a retrospective quote, not a claimed 2009 live utterance.
  An earlier paragraph names Hal's prior RPOW experiment.
- https://www.metzdowd.com/pipermail/cryptography/2009-January/014994.html
  Satoshi's first release announcement, titled Bitcoin v0.1 released, with a
  bitcoin-0.1.0.rar link. Supports the terminal version clue; this earlier release
  date must not replace the transfer's block date.
