## The moment

Lightning Labs released lnd 0.4-beta, its first Lightning implementation designated for Bitcoin mainnet use.

**Why it earns a card:** The payment-channel vision reaches an important mainnet milestone.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A pair of users exchange small payments along bright side channels while the main Bitcoin rail remains below them. A newly opened station communicates the arrival of a usable beta, not the birth of every underlying idea.

**Composition:** Layered bridge view; the side route is visually light and the base rail stays structurally important.

**Mood:** Fast, practical possibility; controlled electric blue over warm Bitcoin light.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not promise free or instant settlement in all cases, portray a replacement chain, or equate this release with final maturity.
