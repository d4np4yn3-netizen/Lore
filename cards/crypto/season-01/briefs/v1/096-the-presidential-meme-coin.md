## The moment

President-elect Donald Trump promoted the official TRUMP token in a Truth Social post on 17 January 2025, three days before his inauguration. The token was issued on Solana.

**Why it earns a card:** A sitting-president-to-be launches a personal meme token before inauguration.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A president-elect's branded meme token appears on social feeds days before inauguration, merging political attention with speculative token culture. Show an announcement being seen, not a state-backed currency being issued.

**Composition:** A phone announcement in foreground, a distant ceremonial venue kept symbolic and secondary.

**Mood:** Surprise, controversy and spectacle; restrained patriotic colours against market-screen light.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not call it legal tender, a government reserve asset or official national currency; distinguish US-local announcement time from the following UTC date.
