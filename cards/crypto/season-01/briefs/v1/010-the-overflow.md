## The moment

A value-overflow flaw was exploited in block 74,638 to create roughly 184.47 billion invalid BTC; developers rapidly patched the software and the valid chain rejected the bad history.

**Why it earns a card:** A near-catastrophic bug and rapid repair in Bitcoin's infancy.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A developer catches a numerical overflow before a corrupted wall of blocks overwhelms the room. One valid chain is being repaired while a monstrous false balance dissolves away.

**Composition:** A sharply angled counter and a single corrective action dominate; use the false chain as a looming background, not pages of code.

**Mood:** Urgent problem-solving; sickly overflow light against clean restored blue.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not suggest the invalid coins remain in today's supply or portray the rescue as one person's unsupported solo achievement.
