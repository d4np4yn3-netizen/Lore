## The moment

Larva Labs launched CryptoPunks in June 2017 as 10,000 algorithmically generated characters on Ethereum, initially claimable for free.

**Why it earns a card:** A defining early profile-picture collection.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A wall of distinct pixel portraits opens to early collectors, each face small yet individually memorable. The focal figure discovers a character in the grid, capturing identity and scarcity before speculative grandeur.

**Composition:** A human-scale gallery wall bends into depth; a handful of original, non-copied study portraits anchors the foreground.

**Mood:** Curiosity and subcultural energy; flat colours within the locked illustrated style.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not use later market prices as launch facts, merge version-one and version-two dates, or depict all 10,000 portraits as manually painted originals.
