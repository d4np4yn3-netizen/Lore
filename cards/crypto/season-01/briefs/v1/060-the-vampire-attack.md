## The moment

SushiSwap launched as a Uniswap fork with SUSHI liquidity-mining incentives and then migrated more than $800 million of liquidity away from Uniswap in a 'vampire attack.'

**Why it earns a card:** A memorable competitive liquidity migration.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A playful sushi counter offers incentives that persuade liquidity providers to move assets from a neighbouring exchange pool. Make the movement voluntary and strategic: the vampire image is a metaphor for competition.

**Composition:** Two adjacent pools connected by a clearly open migration route, with providers carrying trays themselves.

**Mood:** Mischievous rivalry; magenta and warm kitchen light.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not depict a hack, stolen user funds, literal vampires attacking people, or every stage of the episode as occurring on one day.
