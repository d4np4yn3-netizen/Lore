## The moment

New York's Department of Financial Services made its virtual-currency regulation, commonly called the BitLicense, effective on 24 June 2015.

**Why it earns a card:** A major early attempt to license crypto businesses.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A New York street of emerging crypto businesses meets a newly installed licensing gate. Some operators approach with folders while others study routes around it, making the trade-off visible without assigning villains.

**Composition:** Street-level view with the gate offset; multiple small businesses remain legible beyond it.

**Mood:** Institutional pressure; limestone grey with pockets of startup colour.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not imply the rule covered every global user, invent closures of specific firms, or depict it as a federal law.
