## The moment

Mike Caldwell began producing Casascius physical bitcoins: funded Bitcoin private keys embedded beneath tamper-evident holograms on physical coins.

**Why it earns a card:** A physical collectible that makes digital bearer value tangible.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A collector studies a physical Bitcoin coin and its intact security seal. Make the fascination tactile while keeping the distinction between the metal object and the private key clear.

**Composition:** Macro three-quarter view of fingertips, coin edge and seal; a soft display-case background keeps the silhouette uncluttered.

**Mood:** Tactile wonder; brushed metal and carefully drawn holographic light.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not expose a usable private key or imply that every similar souvenir coin contains funded Bitcoin.
