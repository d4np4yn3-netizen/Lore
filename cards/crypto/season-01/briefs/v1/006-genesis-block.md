## The moment

Bitcoin's genesis block was timestamped 3 January 2009 and embedded the Times headline about a possible second bank bailout.

**Why it earns a card:** The beginning of Bitcoin's chain and its defining newspaper reference.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A lone first block sits in a vast unfinished network while a newspaper fragment is embedded in its foundation. The scene should feel like the beginning of something improbable, with no visible identity assigned to Satoshi.

**Composition:** Low viewpoint at the first block; faint future block positions recede upward, leaving the main block unmistakable.

**Mood:** Solemn beginning; dawn light breaking through cold architectural shadow.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not identify Satoshi, fabricate a physical mining site or label the embedded headline as a proven statement of personal motive.
