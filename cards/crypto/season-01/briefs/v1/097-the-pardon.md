## The moment

President Donald Trump granted Ross William Ulbricht a pardon on 21 January 2025. Ulbricht had been serving a life sentence following his 2015 conviction in the Silk Road case.

**Why it earns a card:** The Silk Road founder's pardon closes an important chapter of crypto history.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A signed presidential pardon changes the legal fate of Ross Ulbricht after years in prison. Centre the dated legal document and an opening threshold, avoiding a victory declaration about the underlying conduct.

**Composition:** Document in foreground, a symbolic open doorway beyond; any portrait requires an approved reference.

**Mood:** Relief and contested legacy; quiet daylight after institutional shadow.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not equate a pardon with a judicial finding of innocence, erase the conviction history, or depict a specific release scene without evidence.
