## The moment

Hayden Adams publicly announced and deployed Uniswap to Ethereum mainnet on 2 November 2018.

**Why it earns a card:** A simple exchange mechanism becomes core crypto infrastructure.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** Two token reservoirs feed an open automated exchange in a modest builder's workshop. A trader turns a simple handle and sees assets change sides without a traditional order-book counterparty desk.

**Composition:** Low view across the twin pools, with the builder secondary and the mechanism visually simple.

**Mood:** Resourceful invention; clean magenta highlights and workshop warmth.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not depict the UNI token at the 2018 launch, imply price cannot move, or suggest all later versions share an identical mechanism.
