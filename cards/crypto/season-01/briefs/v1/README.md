Four clue entries retain details described in existing approved-card records; the other 396 are proposed additions or new-card concepts. No artwork was generated or edited.

Other prior holds outside the list are unaffected.

Historical references, artistic symbolism and cross-card callbacks are labelled separately. Sources are inherited research leads, not a claim that all 400 details have just been independently verified. Exact final copy and detailed factual props require focused checks.
