## The moment

Compound began distributing COMP governance tokens to protocol users who supplied or borrowed assets.

**Why it earns a card:** COMP distribution helps set off the liquidity-mining boom.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A lending market becomes a summer allotment where supplying and borrowing both release governance-token rewards. The surprise is how incentives change behaviour and draw crowds to the same plot.

**Composition:** Two clear paths enter a shared lending garden; rewards sit on a separate shelf, distinct from interest.

**Mood:** Excitement with visible complexity; saturated green and violet.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not portray risk-free yield, confuse governance tokens with deposited principal, or imply one date accounts for all of DeFi Summer.
