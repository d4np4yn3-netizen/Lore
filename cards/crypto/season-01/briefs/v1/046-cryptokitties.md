## The moment

Axiom Zen launched CryptoKitties publicly on 28 November 2017.

**Why it earns a card:** Collectible creatures bring blockchain congestion into popular culture.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** Digital cats multiply through a whimsical breeding workshop until deliveries overflow the surrounding Ethereum street. Keep the creatures expressive and the congestion understandable at a glance.

**Composition:** One cat in the foreground, paired incubator shapes behind, and a queue reaching into the distance.

**Mood:** Delight turning into comic overload; pastel warmth against violet infrastructure.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not imply the entire chain permanently stopped, copy protected cat artwork without reference approval, or describe breeding as biological fact.
