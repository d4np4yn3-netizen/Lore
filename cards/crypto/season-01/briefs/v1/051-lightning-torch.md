## The moment

The Lightning Torch began on 19 January 2019 and was passed between hundreds of participants across dozens of countries before reaching its protocol/payment limit.

**Why it earns a card:** A relay of payments creates a participatory global story.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A Lightning payment moves hand to hand as a glowing relay torch around a table that bends like a world map. The focus is trust among strangers and the practical limits of a shared experiment.

**Composition:** Circular handoff composition with one clear current holder; distant hands fade into map contours.

**Mood:** Friendly suspense; warm flame inside cool blue channels.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not claim every country participated, draw a literal Olympic event, or show an unlimited payment size.
