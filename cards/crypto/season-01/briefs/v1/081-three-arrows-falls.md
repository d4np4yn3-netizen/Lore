## The moment

Crypto hedge fund Three Arrows Capital entered insolvency proceedings in mid-2022 after heavy losses and leveraged exposures.

**Why it earns a card:** A major fund's collapse spreads leverage-related contagion.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** An overextended investment firm's three-column structure loses its supports, sending strain through connected lending desks. Focus on leverage and counterparty dependence rather than an invented scene of founders fleeing.

**Composition:** Three tilted structural columns dominate, with fine connecting lines reaching smaller desks below.

**Mood:** Cascading uncertainty; slate grey and stressed crimson accents.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not invent a single collapse day, depict named individuals committing an unsourced act, or imply every connected lender suffered identical losses.
