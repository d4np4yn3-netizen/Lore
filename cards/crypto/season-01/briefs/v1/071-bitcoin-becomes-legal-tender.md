## The moment

El Salvador's Bitcoin Law entered into force in September 2021, making Bitcoin legal tender alongside the US dollar under the law then in effect.

**Why it earns a card:** El Salvador's law takes effect, a defining sovereign-adoption moment.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** An ordinary shop in El Salvador displays both dollar and bitcoin payment choices on the day the law takes effect. Centre everyday use and uncertainty rather than a leader portrait or utopian city.

**Composition:** Shop-counter view with customer and merchant considering two distinct payment routes.

**Mood:** Historic experiment grounded in daily life; sunlit colour with thoughtful expressions.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not imply everyone used bitcoin willingly, confuse June passage with September effect, or describe the 2021 regime as unchanged today.
