## The moment

A US executive order established a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve capitalized initially with finally forfeited government BTC and directed that Bitcoin deposited into the reserve not be sold.

**Why it earns a card:** An executive order establishes a federal strategic Bitcoin reserve.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** An executive order establishes a US strategic bitcoin reserve using eligible forfeited holdings as its initial basis. Show an inventory being redesignated, with other digital assets kept in a separate stockpile file.

**Composition:** Government records room with a newly labelled bitcoin ledger and a distinct secondary asset cabinet.

**Mood:** Institutional consequence; sober navy and restrained amber.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not depict an automatic market-buying spree, a new national digital currency, or all seized assets as already finally forfeited and freely usable.
