## The moment

MakerDAO's first production Dai system went live on Ethereum on 18 December 2017, initially backed by Ether.

**Why it earns a card:** A decentralised dollar mechanism becomes usable.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A small stablecoin workshop balances a dollar-denominated token against a visibly larger stack of ether collateral. Stability is a maintained arrangement with buffers and mechanisms, not a miracle.

**Composition:** A sturdy balance bench fills the frame; collateral, output and a maintenance gauge remain distinct.

**Mood:** Careful engineering; cream paper, violet collateral and muted brass.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not put later multi-collateral assets into the initial release or imply the token could never lose its peg.
