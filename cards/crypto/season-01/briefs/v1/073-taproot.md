## The moment

Taproot's new Bitcoin consensus rules activated at block 709,632 after locking in through the Speedy Trial activation process.

**Why it earns a card:** A substantial Bitcoin upgrade changes signature and scripting capabilities.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** Bitcoin's upgrade appears as a carefully grafted new branch on an established cryptographic tree. Existing roots remain intact while selected spending paths become more elegant and flexible.

**Composition:** Close view of the graft with the full sturdy trunk visible; no giant unrelated growth explosion.

**Mood:** Patient craftsmanship; muted green and amber.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not promise universal transaction anonymity, depict a separate coin launch, or imply every user instantly adopted the new spending type.
