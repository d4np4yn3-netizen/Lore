## The moment

Chaum presented Blind Signatures for Untraceable Payments at CRYPTO '82, a direct foundation for privacy-preserving electronic cash.

**Why it earns a card:** Introduces the privacy problem digital cash tries to solve.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** An early cryptographer demonstrates private digital cash by signing an opaque envelope while the payment inside stays concealed. The drama is the separation of trust from identity, expressed through a human exchange.

**Composition:** Three-quarter tabletop view; hands and envelope form the focal triangle. Keep faces secondary and the far side of the table in shadow.

**Mood:** Quiet discovery; warm paper against cool terminal light.

**Factual boundaries:** No Bitcoin logos in an ostensibly 1982 room; any later reference must be openly classified as a crossover.
