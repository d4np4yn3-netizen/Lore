## The moment

MicroStrategy announced it had purchased 21,454 BTC for $250 million and adopted Bitcoin as its primary treasury reserve asset.

**Why it earns a card:** MicroStrategy establishes a prominent corporate Bitcoin strategy.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A corporate treasury room replaces part of its familiar cash reserve display with a bitcoin allocation. An ordinary boardroom decision acquires historical weight; avoid an idolised portrait or triumphant price forecast.

**Composition:** A signed allocation folder in the foreground, restrained reserve cabinets beyond.

**Mood:** Deliberate conviction; corporate charcoal with a measured amber accent.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not imply all corporate cash was converted, treat later purchases as part of the first announcement, or give investment advice.
