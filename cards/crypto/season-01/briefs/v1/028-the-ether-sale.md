## The moment

Ethereum launched its 42-day Ether genesis sale on 22 July 2014, accepting Bitcoin and initially offering 2,000 ETH per BTC.

**Why it earns a card:** The funding moment behind Ethereum's launch.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A digital crowdsale desk exchanges bitcoin contributions for promises of ether in a network still under construction. The distant architecture is unfinished: buyers are backing a future platform.

**Composition:** Foreground exchange counter with a scaffolded computational city beyond; leave the future visibly incomplete.

**Mood:** Hopeful construction; indigo night with amber work lights.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not depict live Ethereum applications in July 2014 or imply the initial sale rate lasted through the whole sale.
