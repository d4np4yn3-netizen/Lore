## The moment

QuadrigaCX shut down after founder Gerald Cotten's reported death and entered court-supervised proceedings. Later OSC review concluded the exchange owed clients roughly C$215 million with almost no assets available and that the shortfall resulted largely from Cotten's fraud and mismanagement, not simply inaccessible cold-wallet keys.

**Why it earns a card:** A notorious exchange collapse with an extraordinary missing-key narrative.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** Customers face an exchange safe whose locked-door story does not explain the damaged ledgers behind it. The composition should convey a collapse involving fraud and mismanagement as well as the reported death of its operator.

**Composition:** Foreground customer claim folders, a closed safe to one side, and torn accounting records visible behind it.

**Mood:** Distrust and grief without sensationalism; cold office light.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not reduce the losses to one missing password, allege a faked death, or invent a discovered master key or victim portrait.
