## The moment

Ethereum's Beacon Chain produced its genesis block on 1 December 2020 and began running proof-of-stake consensus alongside the existing proof-of-work chain.

**Why it earns a card:** The proof-of-stake chain lays groundwork for The Merge.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A new proof-of-stake lighthouse begins shining beside Ethereum's still-working mining harbour. The two systems coexist; the viewer can see a future connection but it is not yet made.

**Composition:** Two distinct structures share the coastline, separated by water and an unfinished connecting span.

**Mood:** Patient anticipation; violet dusk and a clear beacon beam.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not show proof of work ending in 2020, claim execution moved to the Beacon Chain at genesis, or present staking as risk-free.
