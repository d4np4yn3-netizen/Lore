## The moment

Six participants generated the Sprout zk-SNARK public parameters through a multi-party trusted-setup ceremony shortly before Zcash launched.

**Why it earns a card:** An unusually tangible ritual behind cryptographic privacy.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** Six separated workstations contribute pieces to a delicate cryptographic construction while each destroys a private remnant. The scene uses a split-room composition to avoid inventing a single shared ceremony room.

**Composition:** One central public structure surrounded by six visually separate compartments; discarded remnants stay secondary.

**Mood:** Ritual tension with laboratory restraint.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not publish secrets, imply all participants met in one room, or describe the ceremony as eliminating every form of trust or risk.
