## The moment

TerraUSD (UST) lost its dollar peg in May 2022 and collapsed toward zero.

**Why it earns a card:** The UST failure is a central stablecoin disaster; existing approved card.

**Date qualification:** Uses the approved card's 9 May milestone within the wider May depeg sequence; this is not a claim that every initial fluctuation began on that day.

**Approved interpretation to preserve:** Fictional city and observer. Shattered moon symbolises LUNA collapse; UST and severed $1 cable symbolise loss of the dollar peg. Anchor sign references advertised interest up to 20%. No real moon, physical reserve/collateral link, literal location or actual person is depicted. 09 MAY 2022 dates the reassurance post during the multi-day collapse.

**Approved date / rarity:** 2022-05-09 / rare.
