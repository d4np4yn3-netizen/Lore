## The moment

ConstitutionDAO raised roughly $47 million from thousands of contributors to bid for a rare first printing of the US Constitution but lost the Sotheby's auction; the document sold for $43.2 million.

**Why it earns a card:** An internet collective attempts to buy a historical document at auction.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** An online crowd pools funds for a rare Constitution auction, then watches the winning lot move beyond reach. Keep the funded crowd and the lost bid in the same story; participation did not guarantee acquisition.

**Composition:** Crowd-lit screens in foreground, a distant auction document behind glass, and a closed return desk at one edge.

**Mood:** Collective ambition followed by disappointment; parchment gold against digital violet.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not say the DAO bought the document, equate gross funds raised with bidding capacity, or show a fictional bidder as the actual winner.
