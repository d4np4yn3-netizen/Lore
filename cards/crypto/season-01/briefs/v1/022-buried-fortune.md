## The moment

In 2013, a discarded hard drive left James Howells unable to access a large Bitcoin holding. His efforts to recover it from a Newport landfill became widely reported in November; the exact disposal date is not established here.

**Why it earns a card:** An enduring human story about losing access to digital wealth.

**Date qualification:** Approved card is year-only. 27 November is a publicity milestone, not a verified date of disposal. No exact disposal day is invented.

**Approved interpretation to preserve:** Poetic landfill cutaway with relative foreground enlargement; not a recovered object or witnessed excavation. Rain, character, machinery, in-scene sign and 2009 tape placement are editorial illustration. A dirty, mostly concealed Doge plush is a fictional visual callback. No value headline appears on the front.

**Approved date / rarity:** 2013 / rare.

**Locked artwork:** [cards/crypto/season-01/buried-fortune-master-01/art.png](https://github.com/d4np4yn3-netizen/Lore/blob/main/cards/crypto/season-01/buried-fortune-master-01/art.png).

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** Preserve the approved poetic landfill cutaway, enlarged discarded drive and searching figure. The story is access to a fortune becoming physically unreachable; this is a symbolic view beneath rubbish, not a documented excavation.

**Composition:** Keep the locked scale contrast and existing foreground drive treatment.

**Mood:** Retain rain, industrial gloom and the small human silhouette.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not show recovered coins, a recovered drive, an exact disposal day, or reinstate the removed newspaper and value headline.
