## The moment

Bitcoin Cash split from Bitcoin after the last common Bitcoin block, height 478,558, on 1 August 2017; BCH then followed rules allowing larger blocks.

**Why it earns a card:** The scaling conflict produces a lasting competing chain.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A crowded Bitcoin road divides into two routes after a long argument over carrying capacity. Both groups continue with shared historical cargo; one route opens wider lanes while the other follows a different scaling path.

**Composition:** View from the common road looking toward the fork; neither branch disappears or becomes a villain's path.

**Mood:** Determined disagreement; amber common ground with diverging accents.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not imply holders had to choose only one resulting balance, call either chain a counterfeit, or depict this as the SegWit activation date.
