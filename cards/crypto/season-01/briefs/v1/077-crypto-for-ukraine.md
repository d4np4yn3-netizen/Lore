## The moment

Ukraine's government said official crypto-donation channels had helped raise more than $53 million by 3 March following Russia's invasion.

**Why it earns a card:** Crypto fundraising becomes part of a real international crisis.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A stream of small digital donations reaches a relief coordination desk during the early response to Russia's invasion of Ukraine. Focus on rapid coordination and practical aid supplies, with no identifiable victims or celebratory combat imagery.

**Composition:** Hands checking incoming contributions at a table, with packed aid boxes beyond a window.

**Mood:** Urgent solidarity; restrained blue and yellow within the established illustration style.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not show a working donation address, imply all reported funds bought particular supplies, or confuse the fundraising milestone with the invasion's start.
