## The moment

Dogecoin entered the weekend of Elon Musk's 8 May 2021 Saturday Night Live appearance after an extraordinary run; Reuters reported it had risen more than 800% in the preceding month and reached an approximately $81 billion market capitalization.

**Why it earns a card:** A memorable collision of a meme coin, celebrity and television.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A television performance becomes a focal point for Dogecoin's mainstream attention. Show viewers watching a stylised late-night set while their phone screens compete for attention; the event is collective anticipation, not a price prophecy.

**Composition:** Television in the upper centre, two fictional viewers in foreground, phones kept secondary.

**Mood:** Anticipatory and absurd; cool screen glow with warm living-room light.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not invent broadcast dialogue, imply a guaranteed price outcome, or show Kabosu physically on the real stage.
