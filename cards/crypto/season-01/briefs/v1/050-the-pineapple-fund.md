## The moment

The anonymous Pineapple Fund completed a large Bitcoin-funded philanthropy campaign across dozens of charities, including multimillion-dollar donations to organizations such as MAPS.

**Why it earns a card:** Balances speculation and loss with a major philanthropy story.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** An anonymous donor sends a harvest of digital wealth toward many small charitable worktables. Keep the donor's face out of view and the recipients varied; generosity, not a mystery identity hunt, is the story.

**Composition:** An open box in the foreground branches into quiet scenes of useful work beyond it.

**Mood:** Warm and grounded; golden light with restrained pineapple-shaped details.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not invent the donor's identity, an exact 2018 start day, a total donation figure, or guaranteed outcomes for beneficiaries.
