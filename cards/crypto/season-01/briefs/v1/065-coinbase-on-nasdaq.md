## The moment

Coinbase Global completed the direct listing of its Class A common stock on Nasdaq on 14 April 2021.

**Why it earns a card:** A crypto exchange reaches a major public stock market.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A crypto exchange enters the public stock market through a direct listing. Employees and ordinary observers watch a new equity ticker appear, while the crypto market remains a distinct layer beyond the glass.

**Composition:** Ticker reflection across a glass entrance, with one small cluster of observers and an opening-day folder.

**Mood:** Institutional arrival; blue daylight with restrained celebration.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not call it an IPO, suggest buyers received bitcoin instead of shares, or invent an opening bell ceremony without photo evidence.
