## The moment

Adam Back's later Hashcash paper states that Hashcash was originally proposed in May 1997 as an anti-spam/proof-of-work mechanism.

**Why it earns a card:** Connects anti-spam proof of work to Bitcoin's ancestry.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A programmer converts repetitive computer work into a small postage-like token, while a flood of unwanted messages slows at the edge of the desk. Make the cost of sending spam understandable through physical action.

**Composition:** Low angle across a desk; one bright stamped message breaks through a darker pile of unwanted mail.

**Mood:** Ingenious and industrious; green terminal glow with restrained copper highlights.

**Factual boundaries:** No claim that Hashcash was a spendable Bitcoin-like currency or required industrial mining rigs.
