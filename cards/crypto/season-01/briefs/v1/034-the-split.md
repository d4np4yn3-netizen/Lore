## The moment

At block 1,920,000, the Ethereum hard fork moved DAO-related Ether to a refund contract; miners opposed to the intervention continued the original chain, which became Ethereum Classic.

**Why it earns a card:** The Ethereum/Ethereum Classic fork embodies a lasting governance question.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A single Ethereum railway reaches a contested junction and continues along two illuminated tracks. Builders stand on both sides with competing plans; the scene should convey a difficult community choice without assigning moral superiority.

**Composition:** Central junction viewed from above; both routes retain visual weight and continue beyond frame.

**Mood:** Sombre resolve; related violet palettes diverge subtly.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not depict two different chains before the fork, a unanimous decision, or an erasure of the original chain.
