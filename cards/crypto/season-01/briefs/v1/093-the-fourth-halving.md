## The moment

At block 840,000, Bitcoin's programmed block subsidy fell from 6.25 BTC to 3.125 BTC.

**Why it earns a card:** The supply schedule reaches a milestone in the ETF era.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A Bitcoin mining conveyor reaches its fourth scheduled reward reduction while the network keeps working. Emphasise a smaller new subsidy beside the ongoing transaction stream rather than a machine cutting existing holdings in half.

**Composition:** Close view of the reward outlet with a broad working network behind it.

**Mood:** Ritual continuity; amber dawn and disciplined mechanical rhythm.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not halve wallet balances, promise a price rise, or use a US-local date without distinguishing it from the UTC date.
