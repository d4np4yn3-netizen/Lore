## The moment

The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission approved exchange rule changes allowing multiple spot Bitcoin exchange-traded products to list and trade.

**Why it earns a card:** US spot Bitcoin products gain a major route to traditional markets.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A regulatory gateway opens for spot-bitcoin exchange-traded products, with trading desks preparing to begin the next day. The focus is a new investment wrapper, not government approval of Bitcoin's merits.

**Composition:** Document gateway in the foreground, unlit exchange terminals waiting beyond it.

**Mood:** Measured institutional arrival; bright paper against cool financial architecture.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not call the order an endorsement of bitcoin, confuse approval with first trading, or assume every product has identical terms or risks.
