## The moment

Attackers stole approximately $1.5 billion in virtual assets from Bybit after compromising a signing workflow associated with an external multisignature service.

**Why it earns a card:** A major exchange theft brings signing and custody risk back into focus.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A custody signing desk approves what appears to be a routine transfer while the underlying transaction route has been maliciously altered. The scene centres a compromised signing workflow rather than a crack in Ethereum itself.

**Composition:** Hands at a multisignature console, with the apparent route and concealed route visually distinct but not executable.

**Mood:** Deception and sudden alarm; cold steel with one misleading green approval light.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not provide exploit instructions, attribute blame to an unsupported individual, or claim Ethereum consensus was broken.
