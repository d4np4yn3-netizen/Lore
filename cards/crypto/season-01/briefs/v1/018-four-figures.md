## The moment

Bitcoin traded above $1,000 for the first time on Mt. Gox on 27 November 2013.

**Why it earns a card:** Captures Bitcoin's first widely recognised four-figure price milestone.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** An early exchange user notices Bitcoin crossing four figures on a modest monitor. Keep the reaction small and believable: surprise rather than a room already full of wealth.

**Composition:** Monitor angled correctly within a domestic trading setup; a foreground hand pauses above the keyboard.

**Mood:** Astonishment; screen light against a quiet room.

**Factual boundaries:** Use the recorded venue and milestone, not a universal claim that every exchange crossed at the same second.
