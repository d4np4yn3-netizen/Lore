## The moment

The first Rare Pepe Counterparty collectible was posted in early September 2016, and the Rare Pepe Directory project was organized days later.

**Why it earns a card:** Internet-native collecting develops its own on-chain culture.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** Collectors trade hand-drawn frog cards at a tiny underground digital bazaar. The centre of attention is a modest collectible and its on-chain provenance, before the later museum-scale NFT market.

**Composition:** Intimate stall view; layered sleeves and a directory binder create places for clues.

**Mood:** Playful, scrappy and proudly niche.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not confuse these Counterparty collectibles with the 2023 PEPE token, imply official artist endorsement, or copy a specific artwork without clearance.
