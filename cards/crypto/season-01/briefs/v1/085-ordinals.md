## The moment

Ordinal inscriptions reached Bitcoin mainnet in January 2023 through Casey Rodarmor's ord software, allowing arbitrary content to be inscribed onto individual satoshis.

**Why it earns a card:** Bitcoin becomes the centre of a new inscription and collecting debate.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** An artist inscribes a small digital artifact onto a satoshi represented as a marked grain moving along Bitcoin's existing chain. The innovation is a new use of familiar infrastructure, with debate visible around it.

**Composition:** Macro view of one engraved grain, widening into a working Bitcoin conveyor and curious observers.

**Mood:** Inventive and contentious; amber with sharp new colour accents.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not show a new blockchain launch, imply physical objects are literally inside coins, or settle contested claims about what Bitcoin should be used for.
