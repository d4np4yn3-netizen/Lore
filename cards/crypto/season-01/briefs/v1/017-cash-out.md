## The moment

A Robocoin kiosk at Waves Coffee House in Vancouver went live as the world's first publicly accessible two-way Bitcoin ATM.

**Why it earns a card:** The first permanent public Bitcoin ATM gives a strong real-world setting.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A customer exchanges cash and Bitcoin at the Robocoin kiosk inside Waves Coffee House. The strongest story is the ordinary cafe around an extraordinary new machine.

**Composition:** Three-quarter view that keeps both kiosk and customer hands believable; a coffee cup provides scale and a shop window adds depth.

**Mood:** Curious and welcoming; rainy Vancouver light outside, warm cafe light within.

**Factual boundaries:** Verify kiosk geometry from contemporary photographs. Do not depict a one-way voucher dispenser or put an impossible perspective on the screen.
