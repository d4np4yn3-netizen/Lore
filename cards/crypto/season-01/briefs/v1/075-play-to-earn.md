## The moment

Axie Infinity grew to millions of players in 2021, while scholarship arrangements let asset owners lend NFT teams to players in exchange for shared earnings.

**Why it earns a card:** Axie brings on-chain gaming into everyday economic life.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A player borrows a team of digital creatures and shares earnings under a scholarship arrangement. Show both the opportunity and dependency: the game screen glows, while the agreement remains on the desk.

**Composition:** Over-shoulder view of a fictional player, with creature silhouettes and a split earnings ledger clearly separated.

**Mood:** Hope mixed with work; humid evening light and screen colour.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not promise sustainable income, portray a real family without reference, or collapse a year-long trend into an invented launch day.
