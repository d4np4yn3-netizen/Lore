## The moment

WikiLeaks publicly announced that it was accepting anonymous Bitcoin donations after conventional payment channels had been restricted.

**Why it earns a card:** A clear early test of censorship-resistant payments.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A donation reaches a publisher through a narrow open network route while conventional payment doors remain closed. Keep the scene about access to payments and public communication, without reproducing sensitive leaked documents.

**Composition:** A human hand sends a modest donation across the foreground; blocked payment entrances form the background context.

**Mood:** Tense but determined; restrained white light through a dark financial corridor.

**Factual boundaries:** No invented internal WikiLeaks scene, leaked personal material or implication that all donations are untraceable.
