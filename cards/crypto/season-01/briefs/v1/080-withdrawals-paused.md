## The moment

Celsius paused all withdrawals, swaps and transfers between customer accounts, citing extreme market conditions.

**Why it earns a card:** Celsius exposes the practical risk of crypto lending platforms.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A customer reaches a lending platform's withdrawal window and finds it suddenly closed while an app still displays a balance. The scene centres the gap between an account number and access to funds.

**Composition:** Hands holding a phone in the foreground, shuttered service window beyond, with a waiting queue fading into shadow.

**Mood:** Frozen anxiety; cold blue with one fading warm light.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not call the June pause a same-day bankruptcy, imply displayed yield was guaranteed, or conflate the platform with a self-custody wallet.
