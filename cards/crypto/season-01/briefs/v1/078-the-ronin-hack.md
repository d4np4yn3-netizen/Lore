## The moment

The Ronin bridge exploit became public on 29 March. Attackers had taken 173,600 ETH and 25.5 million USDC after compromising validator keys.

**Why it earns a card:** A defining bridge failure links gaming adoption to infrastructure risk.

**Date qualification:** Timeline anchors the 29 March disclosure. Contemporary reporting dates the attack to 23 March; the project's later reopening post conflates the dates. Resolve the original incident record before approving a card date for the attack itself.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A bridge guarded by a limited set of signing stations is breached after enough keys fall under attacker control. The public discovery is the focal moment: operators confront the drained route after the attack.

**Composition:** Dark bridge with several compromised gate controls, observed from a later incident-response desk.

**Mood:** Delayed shock; violet night and warning amber.

**Factual boundaries:** Use 29 March as the disclosure anchor; do not silently replace it with the attack date, portray the exploit as gameplay, or reveal usable credentials.
