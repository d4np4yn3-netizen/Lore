## The moment

Bitcoin rose above $100,000 for the first time on 5 December 2024; Reuters reported an intraday high of $103,649.

**Why it earns a card:** Bitcoin crosses the widely recognised $100,000 threshold.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A long-watched bitcoin price display reaches six figures for the first time. A small crowd reacts differently—wonder, disbelief, calculation—while the display remains a historical snapshot rather than a prediction.

**Composition:** Large six-position display reflected in ordinary faces; limit coin spectacle so the number retains meaning.

**Mood:** Astonishment and perspective; warm amber on a dark city evening.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not imply every venue crossed at the same second, use the milestone as today's price, or portray future gains as guaranteed.
