## The moment

Ethereum's Frontier genesis launched on 30 July 2015, bringing the first live Ethereum mainnet into operation.

**Why it earns a card:** Programmable contracts move from plan to functioning mainnet.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** The Ethereum network comes alive like a young city receiving its first power. Builders watch an illuminated chain reach unfinished workspaces, showing the beginning of a platform rather than its mature ecosystem.

**Composition:** Wide upward view; one initial block ignites a branching path through scaffolding.

**Mood:** Dawn, possibility and visible rough edges.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not put later tokens, DAOs or NFT hits into launch-day use; any collection reference must be labelled a flash-forward.
