## The moment

Tesla disclosed in its 2020 Form 10-K that it had invested an aggregate $1.5 billion in bitcoin after updating its investment policy in January 2021.

**Why it earns a card:** A major public company brings Bitcoin onto its balance sheet.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A vehicle-company filing reveals a major bitcoin purchase, turning a routine financial disclosure into a market moment. Centre the document and the distinction between purchase and announcement, with vehicles secondary.

**Composition:** A filing on a desk catches reflected showroom light; an anonymous reader pauses over one highlighted paragraph.

**Mood:** Corporate surprise; white surfaces and restrained amber reflection.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not date the purchase to the disclosure day, imply customers could already pay at this exact moment, or portray the filing as an investment recommendation.
