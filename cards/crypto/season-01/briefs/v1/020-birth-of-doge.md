## The moment

**Why it earns a card:** A joke becomes a lasting community; existing approved card.

**Approved interpretation to preserve:** Kabosu likeness in an invented homemade coin-mint scene. The room, machine and props are fictional; this is not a reconstruction of her home or the founders workplace.

**Approved date / rarity:** 2013-12-06 / epic.
