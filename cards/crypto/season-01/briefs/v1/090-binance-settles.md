## The moment

Binance pleaded guilty to federal charges and agreed to pay more than $4 billion in coordinated US resolutions; founder and CEO Changpeng Zhao separately pleaded guilty to violating the Bank Secrecy Act and resigned.

**Why it earns a card:** A major exchange reaches extensive US government settlements.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A major exchange signs a coordinated legal resolution while its founder's separate plea and departure are recorded in another folder. Keep the institution and individual distinct, with the paperwork carrying the story.

**Composition:** Two separate signing positions on one long table, neither presented as a theatrical courtroom confession.

**Mood:** Consequences and institutional change; sober black, white and muted yellow.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not portray imprisonment on the plea date, merge the company and individual charges, or call the combined resolutions a single unexplained fine.
