## The moment

Hal Finney announced RPOW, a server that exchanged Hashcash proofs for transferable reusable proof-of-work tokens.

**Why it earns a card:** Recognises Hal Finney's pre-Bitcoin contribution.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** Hal Finney's reusable-proof-of-work experiment is imagined as a workshop that accepts a used computational proof and returns a transferable token. Emphasise curiosity and careful construction rather than money raining from a machine.

**Composition:** Hands exchange a token across a compact server; an input chute and return path are visible in one frame.

**Mood:** Patient invention; cool silver hardware and warm human skin tones.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not portray RPOW as Bitcoin, a fully decentralised blockchain, or a documented photograph of Finney's actual room.
