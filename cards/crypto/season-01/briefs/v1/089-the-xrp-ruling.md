## The moment

In SEC v. Ripple Labs, the district court ruled that Ripple's institutional XRP sales under written contracts were securities transactions, while programmatic exchange sales did not satisfy Howey on the record before the court.

**Why it earns a card:** A nuanced court decision becomes a major industry moment.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A court decision splits two types of XRP sales into separate legal folders. The visual point is the distinction in the ruling, not a sweeping victory banner declaring every crypto transaction exempt.

**Composition:** A judge's desk seen without an identifiable face; two folders lead to different annotated outcomes.

**Mood:** Careful relief mixed with complexity; parchment and restrained blue.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not say XRP received blanket immunity, generalise the finding to all tokens or future sales, or treat later proceedings as resolved by this one decision.
