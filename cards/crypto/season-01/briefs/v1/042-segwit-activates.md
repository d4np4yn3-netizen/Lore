## The moment

Segregated Witness activated on Bitcoin mainnet in August 2017 after locking in earlier that month.

**Why it earns a card:** A major Bitcoin upgrade resolves part of the scaling conflict.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A congested block-carrying train is redesigned so witness material sits in a distinct compartment. The train remains one system; the drama is an upgrade that makes later routes possible.

**Composition:** Three-quarter cutaway of a stylised train, with the main cabin and witness compartment both connected and visible.

**Mood:** Ingenious relief; mechanical clarity and warm industrial light.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not show signatures discarded, unlimited block space, or Lightning fully launched at activation.
