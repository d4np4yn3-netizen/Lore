## The moment

Coinbase aired a 60-second bouncing-QR-code Super Bowl ad that generated more than 20 million landing-page hits in one minute and forced the company to throttle traffic briefly.

**Why it earns a card:** A minimalist Super Bowl advert becomes a mainstream crypto moment.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A minimal bouncing square interrupts the spectacle of a major television advert break. Viewers reach for phones as an almost empty screen becomes more memorable than a crowded commercial.

**Composition:** Living-room view with a deliberately simple television rectangle; the moving-square trail gives the composition its rhythm.

**Mood:** Curiosity and playful surprise; saturated screen colour in a dark room.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not include a second functional QR code, recreate a valid signup destination, or imply all visitors became customers.
