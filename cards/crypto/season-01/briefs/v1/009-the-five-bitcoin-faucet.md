## The moment

Gavin Andresen launched a faucet that gave each visitor 5 BTC, initially funding it with 1,100 BTC.

**Why it earns a card:** A memorable contrast with Bitcoin's later scarcity narrative.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A curious early user claims five BTC from a modest website, with the giveaway expressed through a literal tap only as a visual metaphor. The mood is discovery before prices dominate the story.

**Composition:** A small human reaction and an open browser form the focal point; the tap metaphor stays integrated into the desk rather than becoming a separate diagram.

**Mood:** Generous and playful; clear daylight with restrained blue water reflections.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not show the faucet as a current offer or imply that it created new Bitcoin by itself.
