## The moment

Buterin sent roughly 410 trillion SHIB to an inaccessible burn address after receiving an enormous allocation from the token's creators.

**Why it earns a card:** A large unsolicited token allocation meets an unexpected response.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** An enormous unsolicited pile of meme tokens is sent toward an inaccessible address, while a separate charitable gift route remains visibly distinct. The action is a deliberate disposal of token control, not ordinary coin spending.

**Composition:** Two clearly separated paths leave a token pile: the focal path ends at a sealed dark aperture, the smaller path at a donation envelope.

**Mood:** Decisive restraint; charcoal, ember orange and calm violet.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not confuse the burn with the separate charity donation, imply all supply was burned, or display a usable address as a payment instruction.
