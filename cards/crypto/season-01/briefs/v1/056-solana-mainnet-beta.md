## The moment

Solana initialized its Mainnet Beta with a global validator community in March 2020.

**Why it earns a card:** Introduces a major later ecosystem.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A new high-throughput network opens as a coastal transit system with several active stations and temporary beta signs. The story is an ambitious new chain beginning service, with development still visible.

**Composition:** Long curving route through a broad landscape, anchored by one clearly unfinished station.

**Mood:** Bright possibility; mint, violet and early-morning sky.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not imply complete maturity, promise a transaction rate without conditions, or import later meme-token activity into launch month.
