## The moment

Charlie Lee announced Litecoin on 9 October 2011; Litecoin's genesis block was mined about three days later, and the planned public launch time was 13 October.

**Why it earns a card:** An enduring early alternative to Bitcoin.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** Two different mining rhythms run beside each other as Litecoin begins its own path. An ordinary workstation and a human operator should ground the silver metaphor in a real software launch.

**Composition:** A foreground operator starts a second machine; the newer lane diverges gently rather than destroying Bitcoin's lane.

**Mood:** Fresh and optimistic; silver-grey with warm workshop light.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not portray Litecoin as a Bitcoin upgrade, declare a rivalry winner or collapse announcement, genesis and public launch into one timestamp.
