## The moment

**Why it earns a card:** A recognisable internet meme drives another token cycle.

**Mood:** Chaotic meme energy; acid green moderated by dark ink contours.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not imply endorsement by Pepe's original artist, equate the token with Rare Pepe NFTs, or print a later market cap as its launch value.
