# Brief standard — LORE Crypto Season One

Revision v1, 26 September 2026. These are **100 editorial and illustration briefs with 400 Easter egg concepts**, prepared from selection v1 and the existing approved-card register. This pack is not an artwork approval or a claim that every proposed detail has received a new primary-source check.

## Read a brief

Each brief specifies the historical moment, why it earns a card, the scene, composition, mood, factual limits, four discoverable clues, copy status and source leads. The three-digit prefix is review order only, not final collector numbering. Working titles and the 89 new moment choices remain proposals.

Clue labels distinguish **Historical reference** (a fact or theme with an inherited research lead; its physical prop is still invented), **Symbolic detail** (an artistic interpretation), **Collection crossover** (a deliberate reference to another selected moment), and **Detail requiring targeted verification**. A label does not certify source verification. Each clue has its own meaning and location; four date/number variants of the same fact do not satisfy the intent.

Every concept is a proposed hiding place, except the few details explicitly recorded as retained in an already approved image. The approved register describes those retained details; this text-only pass does not claim to have newly inspected all art. A forward crossover is intentionally anachronistic. Keep that distinction in the event-page explanation and never use it as documentary evidence. If Dan removes a crossover, replace it with a different event-specific clue so the eventual card still has at least four approved clues.

## Scene and clue hierarchy

The historical action must read first. Integrate clues into the scene's drawn surfaces, perspective and lighting; no floating explanation labels. Clue 1 should be discoverable at normal viewing size, clue 2 on a closer look, clue 3 as a subtle visual puzzle, and clue 4 as a collection connection or deep cut. These are relative priorities, not a fixed grid or permission to overcrowd the art.

Keep clues clear of title, phrase, badge, QR and cut-risk regions in the existing art window. Avoid microscopic text. If the four details do not survive a finished-size proof, simplify their shapes or move them within a newly reviewed candidate. Final clue visibility requires inspection of actual artwork; this pack cannot certify it. Do not conceal a fifth functional QR, payment destination, private key or credential as a puzzle.

## Locked LORE treatment

Use LORE-CRYPTO-STYLE-v1.1 across the entire image: confident anime/manhwa contours, expressive faces, grouped hair or fur, two or three cel-shaded tones, cinematic light and equally drawn backgrounds and props. Avoid photoreal food/fur, glossy 3D rendering, airbrush mush and generic grunge. Vary the scene, camera and palette by event; do not import the literal dog, man or room from the references into unrelated scenes.

## Dates, phrases and approvals

Retain the selection's exact date precision and qualifications. Year-only, month-only and multi-day events do not acquire fabricated day dates. Distinguish publication, attack, disclosure, filing, implementation and later outcome. Future cards describing historical laws or rulings must identify their historical scope, not imply their legal position is unchanged today.

For the 11 visually approved cards, preserve the exact art, title, phrase, date, rarity, placement and files. Four clue entries do not mean four new details have been inserted. Optional additions require a separate proposed revision and Dan's visual approval. Existing source-note qualifications continue to apply. Visual approval is separate from phrase verification and print release.

For the other 89 cards, the brief's prose is editorial guidance, **not a final printed moment phrase**. Select a short authentic excerpt from the specific original post, paper, recording or announcement; record exact location/timestamp and a source-backed shortlist for Dan. If no suitable phrase can be verified, disclose that and agree an editorial alternative. Never silently invent a quotation. Do not assign new rarities, collector numbers, edition quantities or foil odds here.

First Transfer and First Halving remain paused. Existing parked Slush Pool and Silk Road concepts remain parked. Writing their briefs does not authorise restarting their artwork. Other parked concepts outside this selected 100, including Dogecar, remain untouched.

## Evidence and economical next steps

Sources and event summaries are inherited from the saved year-shard research and selection v1, with existing uncertainty retained. No exhaustive new source audit or numerical event ranking was performed for this writing pass. The clue explanations are research directions where their details go beyond that source summary. Exact dates, numbers, quotations, products and legal/technical depictions need targeted checks before final copy/art; do not restart research on all 1,229 archive candidates.

Review the chosen moment and four clues, resolve only its specific evidence gaps, approve the brief/copy, then make and review one new card at a time. Artwork, final copy, evidence, QR routing, physical proof and print release are separate statuses. No asset, demo QR, existing print-release flag or approved register is changed by this pack.
