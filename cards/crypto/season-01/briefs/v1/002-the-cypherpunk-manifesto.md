## The moment

Eric Hughes published A Cypherpunk's Manifesto to the Cypherpunks mailing list.

**Why it earns a card:** Gives the movement a clear human philosophy and voice.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A late-night group shares a newly written privacy manifesto over a mailing-list terminal. Show conviction and practical work: one person types while another passes a sealed note into the room.

**Composition:** Over-shoulder view with a readable human gesture; use a doorway and cables to suggest a wider unseen community.

**Mood:** Defiant, intimate and constructive; amber desk light and deep blue shadows.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not invent a documented meeting, named attendees or a source quotation.
