## The moment

Arbitrum opened claims for its ARB governance-token airdrop on 23 March 2023 as part of the transition to the Arbitrum DAO.

**Why it earns a card:** A large rollup community receives governance tokens.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** Users of an Ethereum scaling network open governance allocations while the network's rollup machinery continues in the background. The milestone is the token claim and governance transition, not the launch of the underlying network itself.

**Composition:** A community meeting table sits in front of an already-operating rollup station.

**Mood:** Participation and transition; cool blue with restrained celebratory colour.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not imply every wallet qualified, describe ARB as Ethereum itself, or equate receiving governance tokens with ownership of all protocol revenue.
