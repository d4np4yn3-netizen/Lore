## The moment

The US President signed S. 1582, the Guiding and Establishing National Innovation for U.S. Stablecoins Act (GENIUS Act), into law.

**Why it earns a card:** US stablecoin legislation becomes a federal milestone.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A federal payment-stablecoin framework is signed into law, creating a rulebook around a specific class of digital money. Keep the book's scope visibly narrower than the entire crypto market and its rollout unfinished.

**Composition:** Signed law in foreground, a stablecoin payment desk beyond, with a partly assembled implementation calendar.

**Mood:** A new regulatory chapter; cream, navy and measured green.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not say all crypto was approved, all stablecoins instantly complied, or every provision became operational on the signing day.
