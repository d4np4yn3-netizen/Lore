## The moment

Ethereum's official history dates the introductory whitepaper to 27 November 2013; Vitalik Buterin had circulated the first draft to prospective collaborators that month.

**Why it earns a card:** Introduces the broader programmable-blockchain ambition.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A young developer's design paper unfolds into many possible applications rather than only a payment route. Treat the room as an interpreted writing scene and the author as source-referenced, not a photographic reconstruction.

**Composition:** Paper and hands in the foreground; a small branching model of applications rises behind, with a clear single focal direction.

**Mood:** Expansive curiosity; pale violet and warm desk light.

**Factual boundaries:** No claim to know the exact room or the author's thoughts; avoid representing applications as already launched in 2013.
