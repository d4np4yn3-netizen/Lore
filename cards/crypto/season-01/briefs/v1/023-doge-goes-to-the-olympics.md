## The moment

The Dogecoin community raised more than 26 million DOGE, worth roughly $25,000 at the time, to help the Jamaican bobsled team reach the Sochi Winter Olympics.

**Why it earns a card:** Community fundraising creates a memorable sporting crossover.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A Jamaican bobsled waits at the edge of an icy track while a stream of small community donations becomes the last push it needs. Keep the athletes capable and central; the crypto crowd supports their journey.

**Composition:** Low diagonal along the sled, with the runners leading toward bright ice. Donation details live on equipment and travel gear.

**Mood:** Joyful collective effort; tropical accents against cold blue ice.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not imply Dogecoin paid every team expense or determined the Olympic result; avoid invented athlete portraits.
