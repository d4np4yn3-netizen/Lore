## The moment

FTX Trading and more than 100 affiliated entities filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in Delaware on 11 November 2022.

**Why it earns a card:** The defining exchange collapse of the 2022 crisis.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** An exchange's bright trading frontage goes dark while customers gather claim documents outside. Through the glass, a tangle of affiliated-company folders suggests the scale of the bankruptcy without pretending the illustration proves every allegation.

**Composition:** Customer-level view of a closed exchange facade, with paperwork and an extinguished sign as the focal elements.

**Mood:** Betrayal and uncertainty; cold cyan fading into darkness.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not date every revelation or arrest to 11 November, invent a criminal conviction on filing day, or reduce all affiliated entities to one legal company.
