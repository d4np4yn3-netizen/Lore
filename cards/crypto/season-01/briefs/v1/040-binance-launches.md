## The moment

Binance launched its exchange on 14 July 2017 after a June-to-July BNB token sale.

**Why it earns a card:** Marks the arrival of a major exchange institution.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A new exchange launches as a brightly lit trading concourse assembling itself around arriving users. Make speed of growth and a new marketplace the narrative, without borrowing the imagery of a securities exchange debut.

**Composition:** Diagonal concourse view with a modest opening desk in front and many emerging routes beyond.

**Mood:** Startup urgency; dark space punctuated by yellow work lights.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not portray a public-stock listing, a mature global footprint on day one, or later legal events as part of the opening.
