## The moment

During the global COVID-19 market panic, Bitcoin fell violently alongside traditional risk assets, dropping below $6,000 and later below $5,000 during the wider selloff.

**Why it earns a card:** A defining cross-market crash during the pandemic.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A global market storm sweeps through a crypto trading room during the early pandemic sell-off. Falling screens and strained liquidation machinery share the frame; there is no single villain pulling a lever.

**Composition:** Wide room with one small observer and several linked markets visible through windows.

**Mood:** Alarm and disorientation; cyan screens under storm-grey light.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not attribute every decline to one cause, use a later day's low as this day's exact price, or show a fictional trader as a real victim.
