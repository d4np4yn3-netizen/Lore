## The moment

Kevin McCoy's digital artwork Quantum was originally minted on the Namecoin blockchain on 3 May 2014.

**Why it earns a card:** A distinctive early connection between blockchain and digital art.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** An artist registers an animated geometric work on an early blockchain while its luminous forms escape a small screen. Focus on the leap from a digital image to a recorded claim, not an auction spectacle.

**Composition:** Intimate studio view; a polygonal glow illuminates the artist's hands and a modest terminal.

**Mood:** Experimental and handmade; restrained violet and amber.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not call it the uncontested first NFT, use later Ethereum interfaces, or reproduce the full artwork without an approved reference.
