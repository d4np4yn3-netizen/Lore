## The moment

Chainlink launched on Ethereum mainnet as a decentralized oracle network connecting smart contracts to off-chain data.

**Why it earns a card:** Connects contracts to external information.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A smart-contract workshop receives carefully routed information from the outside world. Several independent couriers converge on a verification desk before data enters a sealed on-chain machine.

**Composition:** Split exterior/interior scene; the data crossing is central and the token imagery secondary.

**Mood:** Connection and cautious trust; cobalt with warm paper signals.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not imply every external claim becomes true automatically or treat oracle networks as a cure for all bad data.
