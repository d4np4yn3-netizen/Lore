## The moment

Andre Cronje launched the YFI governance token with no pre-mine, investor sale or founder allocation, distributing the initial 30,000 tokens through liquidity provision.

**Why it earns a card:** An emblematic community-led token distribution and yield-farming story.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A community finds a small, initially limited batch of governance tokens left on an open workshop table. There is no privileged founder's throne; the scene captures the unusual initial distribution and collective rush.

**Composition:** Top-down table with ordinary participants around all sides, no central owner figure.

**Mood:** Improvised collaboration; blue ink, warm wood and evening light.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not describe the initial 30,000 supply as an eternal cap, imply no later governance changes, or promise holders investment returns.
