## The moment

The US Marshals Service auctioned 29,656 BTC forfeited from Silk Road infrastructure on 27 June 2014.

**Why it earns a card:** Seized digital money enters a government auction.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** An auctioneer's gavel hovers above sealed bid envelopes while forfeited bitcoin becomes an unusual government auction lot. Use a fictional administrative room with the public process, rather than a glamorous winner, as the subject.

**Composition:** High table-level view; sealed envelopes form the foreground and the gavel holds the centre.

**Mood:** Sober, procedural and faintly surreal.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not portray physical bitcoin coins as the actual assets, identify an invented bidder, or confuse this lot with later seizures.
