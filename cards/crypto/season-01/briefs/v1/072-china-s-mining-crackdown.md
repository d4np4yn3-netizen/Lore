## The moment

On 24 September, authorities published a nationwide mining notice dated 3 September 2021. This formalised a crackdown already under way earlier that year.

**Why it earns a card:** A major intervention reshapes Bitcoin's mining geography.

**Date qualification:** 24 September is publication of a notice dated 3 September. Earlier May/June restrictions and mining migration must not be presented as beginning in September.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** Mining equipment is packed into transit crates as official restrictions reshape China's mining industry. The composition combines an administrative notice with relocation, making clear that the public notice formalised a process already under way.

**Composition:** Warehouse foreground with unplugged rigs and open shipping crates; the notice is secondary but visible.

**Mood:** Dislocation and adaptation; industrial grey with distant sunrise.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not date the entire crackdown to one September day, claim all mining ended forever, or confuse mining restrictions with a worldwide Bitcoin shutdown.
