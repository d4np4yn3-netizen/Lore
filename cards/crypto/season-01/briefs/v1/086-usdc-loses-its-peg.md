## The moment

After Silicon Valley Bank failed, Circle disclosed that $3.3 billion of USDC reserves were held at the bank; USDC traded below $1 over the weekend before recovering after US authorities protected depositors.

**Why it earns a card:** Banking risk briefly destabilises a major dollar token.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A stablecoin reserve file is trapped behind a failed bank's closed window while the token's dollar gauge briefly drifts. A later reopening glow at the edge signals recovery without erasing the weekend's uncertainty.

**Composition:** Bank window and reserve ledger in the foreground, separate peg gauge to the side.

**Mood:** Temporary alarm easing toward relief; bank-grey and blue-green.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not portray USDC as algorithmic like UST, claim all reserves were at Silicon Valley Bank, or present the brief depeg as permanent.
