## The moment

Trezor Model One officially launched on 29 July 2014 as the first commercial hardware wallet.

**Why it earns a card:** A strong physical object for the self-custody story.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A person protects a small hardware wallet in one hand while a compromised-looking computer looms in the other half of the room. The story is isolating signing keys from the everyday machine.

**Composition:** Close view of hands and device, with the monitor pushed into the background; no readable secret on screen.

**Mood:** Reassuring control; clean warm foreground against cool computer glare.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not expose a usable seed, imply assets sit inside the device, or draw a modern model as the 2014 Model One.
