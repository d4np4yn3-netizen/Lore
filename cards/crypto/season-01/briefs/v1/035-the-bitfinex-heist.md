## The moment

Bitfinex suffered a security breach on 2 August 2016 in which 119,755 BTC were stolen.

**Why it earns a card:** A major custody failure with a later recovery story.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A trading desk faces a breached reserve vault while customers see balances suddenly become uncertain. Show the human shock and exchange-level failure, with Bitcoin's wider network continuing in the distance.

**Composition:** Broken vault edge in foreground, a worried customer reflected in a dim trading screen.

**Mood:** Cold shock; emergency red used sparingly.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not portray the Bitcoin protocol as hacked, turn later recovery into a same-day event, or render real credentials or a working exploit.
