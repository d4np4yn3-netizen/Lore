## The moment

Ethereum activated the London upgrade at block 12,965,000, introducing EIP-1559's base-fee mechanism and fee burning.

**Why it earns a card:** Ethereum changes fee mechanics and introduces a base-fee burn.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** Ethereum's fee mechanism gains a visible split: a base portion disappears into a controlled furnace while another route remains available to the block producer. Make a protocol change feel mechanical and precise.

**Composition:** Cutaway fee booth with two distinct exits; the main flame is restrained rather than apocalyptic.

**Mood:** Orderly redesign; amber fire inside violet machinery.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not show every fee burned, promise permanently falling supply, or depict this as the end of mining.
