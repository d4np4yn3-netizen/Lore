## The moment

The Monero blockchain went live on 18 April 2014 as a fair-launched CryptoNote-derived privacy cryptocurrency.

**Why it earns a card:** Makes privacy a central thread of the collection.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A sender and recipient exchange value through a crowded night market while identifying trails dissolve among many possible routes. Privacy should feel like ordinary people gaining personal space, without implying criminality.

**Composition:** Two figures separated by layered awnings; the eye can follow several plausible paths but no single glowing surveillance line.

**Mood:** Quiet confidence; copper light and muted charcoal.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not attribute later privacy upgrades to launch day or promise complete anonymity in every circumstance.
