## The moment

The Mt. Gox rehabilitation trustee announced that repayments in Bitcoin and Bitcoin Cash had been made to some rehabilitation creditors through designated exchanges.

**Why it earns a card:** A long-running collapse gains a partial resolution a decade later.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** An old creditor opens a repayment notice a decade after an exchange's collapse. A partial queue of envelopes moves through designated exchanges, while many files remain on the table; the story is the beginning of repayments.

**Composition:** A worn original claim folder beneath a fresh notice, with a modest human reaction rather than a victory crowd.

**Mood:** Long-awaited movement with unresolved weight; rain-grey warmed by morning light.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not imply every creditor was fully repaid on 5 July, call these newly mined coins, or merge initial missing amounts with repayment totals.
