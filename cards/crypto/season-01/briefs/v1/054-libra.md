## The moment

Facebook announced plans for Calibra, a digital wallet intended to support Libra, a proposed global blockchain-based currency.

**Why it earns a card:** Facebook's proposed currency forces a global policy response.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A vast social network unveils a proposed global currency model on a conference table while regulators gather at the doors. Keep the model visibly a proposal rather than a functioning world payment system.

**Composition:** Large unlit globe-model above a small launch presentation, with questioning silhouettes beyond the glass.

**Mood:** Ambition meeting scrutiny; polished blue drained by cool institutional light.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not show Libra circulating as launched global money, confuse its initial basket proposal with later designs, or assert every partner remained involved.
