## The moment

Marek 'Slush' Palatinus announced a cooperative mining pool, widely recognized as the first public Bitcoin mining pool.

**Why it earns a card:** The move from solitary mining toward organised pools.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** Retain the parked Slush Pool art state. A future concept could show ordinary miners bringing separate streams of work into one shared mechanism, with small regular payouts replacing a single rare jackpot.

**Composition:** Several modest workstations converge toward one pool; keep one foreground operator as the human focus and avoid a wall of near-identical GPUs.

**Mood:** Cooperative and practical; warm individual lamps feeding cool shared light.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not restart the rejected concept or treat the pool's announcement as proof of the exact first pooled block time.
