## The moment

The first USDT tokens were issued on the Bitcoin Omni layer on 6 October 2014, according to Tether's anniversary account. The project had begun under the Realcoin name and was subsequently renamed Tether.

**Why it earns a card:** Tether's origins help explain the later stablecoin economy.

**Date qualification:** Selected moment is first issuance on 6 October, as stated in Tether's anniversary post; the source record covers the broader July–November origin sequence. The later Tether name change is not assigned this date.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A dollar-shaped token travels along an early Bitcoin-based payment rail, promising familiar denomination inside an unfamiliar system. Show the token as a claim represented digitally, not a dollar magically created from mining.

**Composition:** Side-on rail or conveyor crossing between a paper ledger and a network terminal.

**Mood:** Practical invention with an undercurrent of trust; teal and cream.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not portray audited one-to-one reserves as independently established by this brief, use a modern chain for the initial launch, or conflate the October launch with the later rename.
