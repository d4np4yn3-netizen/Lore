## The moment

Uniswap launched the UNI governance token and allocated 15% of the genesis supply for immediate distribution to historical users and liquidity providers.

**Why it earns a card:** A token distribution becomes a shared user memory.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** Past users of an exchange discover governance allocations waiting at familiar workshop doors. The scene is surprise ownership and participation, with different recipient roles visible rather than a generic rain of free money.

**Composition:** One recipient opens a modest claim envelope while other doors reveal varied users and liquidity providers.

**Mood:** Unexpected inclusion; bright magenta against an ordinary morning.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not imply every wallet qualified, print an unverified allocation formula, or frame an airdrop as cost-free guaranteed profit.
