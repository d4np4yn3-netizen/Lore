## The moment

An attacker exploited a recursive-call/reentrancy vulnerability in The DAO and began draining Ether into a child DAO.

**Why it earns a card:** A foundational smart-contract failure; existing approved card.

**Approved interpretation to preserve:** Fictional sealed vaults visualise The DAO exploit. A one-way violet stream depicts ETH diverted into the locked child DAO. A small amber loop at the SPLIT control represents recursive calls, not funds returned to The DAO. Enlarged fictional engineers convey urgency. The receiver display references the approximate 27-day withdrawal restriction reported during the attack. No actual physical building, identifiable attacker, Ethereum consensus breach or later July fork is depicted.

**Approved date / rarity:** 2016-06-17 / epic.

**Locked artwork:** [cards/crypto/season-01/the-dao-hack-master-01/art.png](https://github.com/d4np4yn3-netizen/Lore/blob/main/cards/crypto/season-01/the-dao-hack-master-01/art.png).
