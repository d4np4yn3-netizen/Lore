## The moment

Chinese financial regulators declared token fundraising/ICOs an unauthorized and illegal public-financing activity and ordered existing offerings to unwind.

**Why it earns a card:** A major regulatory shock during token-sale mania.

## Illustration brief

**Scene:** A token fundraising fair closes abruptly as an official notice reaches the entrance. Founders and purchasers read it together amid half-packed booths; the moment is a regulatory interruption to a crowded market.

**Composition:** Foreground notice reflected in a shutter, with subdued stalls receding behind it.

**Mood:** Abrupt silence after noise; faded festival colour under grey daylight.

**Factual boundaries:** Do not claim the notice outlawed ownership of every cryptocurrency, confuse ICO restrictions with later mining policy, or invent quotations.
