# 049 - Sources and artwork

Hayden Adams's [A Short History of Uniswap](https://blog.uniswap.org/uniswap-history) confirms the public announcement and Ethereum-mainnet launch on 2 November 2018, the final day of Devcon 4 in Prague.

The archived [V1 overview](https://github.com/Uniswap/v1-docs/blob/3d71d5d7e0ffefd08596de29b7630fe9744721d2/README.md), [pool-liquidity guide](https://github.com/Uniswap/v1-docs/blob/3d71d5d7e0ffefd08596de29b7630fe9744721d2/frontend-integration/pool.md) and [exchange contract](https://github.com/Uniswap/v1-contracts/blob/master/contracts/uniswap_exchange.vy) support the ETH/ERC20 reserve pairing, factory, routing through ETH, constant-product model, liquidity-provider shares, deposits/withdrawals and trading fee. The reserve product is not invariant across fees and liquidity changes. [Introducing UNI](https://blog.uniswap.org/uni) dates the later governance-token introduction to September 2020.

The unicorn, hall and engine are an invented allegory, not a portrait, documented physical event, technical diagram or official Uniswap logo. Four clues: paired reserves, formula, V1 badge and launch date. LIQUIDITY, PAIRED. is the approved editorial caption, not a quotation.
