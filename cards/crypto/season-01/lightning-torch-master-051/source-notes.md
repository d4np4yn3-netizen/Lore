# 051 Lightning Torch source notes

## Historical account

The CoinGecko Q1 2019 report is a contemporary participant account. Printed page 26 records the 19 January start, Hodlonaut, the initial 100,000-satoshi payment and the requested 10,000-satoshi increments. Printed page 27 gives the 83-day span through 11 April, 291 hops, 281 unique recipients and 56 countries, and records two theft interruptions and one exceptional increase. Printed page 29 describes CoinGecko's own handoff. Story totals are attributed to this report and are not presented as a fresh audit of the relay.

Source: https://assets.coingecko.com/reports/2019-Q1-Report/CoinGecko-2019-Q1-Report.pdf#page=26

The technical paragraph uses the Lightning Network's own overview of payment channels and routing. It does not claim the relay was a base-layer transaction at each hop or that every payment met a fixed speed or fee.

Source: https://lightning.network/

## Artwork and wording

The harbour is Portofino-inspired fictional imagery. The people are not identified participants and the boat does not establish an actual journey. The four closeups are native-pixel crops of the exact final art: date tag, starting-amount receipt, globe and travel motifs with Bitcoin pin, and the lantern with its requested increment. The phrase PASS IT ON. is an editorial caption, not an attributed quotation. The increment is described as a requested rule because actual contributions differed.
