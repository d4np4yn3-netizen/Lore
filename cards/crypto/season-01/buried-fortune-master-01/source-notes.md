# Buried Fortune — historical and creative notes

Event: James Howells's accidentally discarded laptop hard drive, Newport, Wales, 2013. Candidate Rare card. Dan accepted the researched concept for drawing with “haha draw the card =)”. Final artwork is awaiting his visual review.

## Evidence used

- [WIRED interview, 1 December 2017](https://www.wired.com/story/bitcoin-lost-newport-landfill/): describes mining on a laptop in 2009, keeping its hard drive, its accidental disposal in 2013, and the inability to search the Newport landfill. Supports the 2009 callback and hardware subject.
- [The Guardian, 3 December 2024](https://www.theguardian.com/uk-news/2024/dec/03/500m-bitcoin-hard-drive-landfill-newport-wales-high-court): the disposal account places the hard drive in a black bag during an office clearout. Supports the bin bag clue.
- [Background and cited references](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitcoin_buried_in_Newport_landfill): identifies Docksway landfill, Newport, and records the discrepancy between early 7,500 BTC reporting and later 8,000 BTC accounts. No quantity or changing fiat valuation appears on this front.

The hard drive held private key material, not physical coins. The exact disposal day is not established, so the existing event-date field reads 2013. ONE DRIVE. A FORTUNE. is editorial wording accepted as part of the concept, not a historical quotation or a new brand slogan.

## Artistic interpretation

The cutaway, rain, character, sign design and staging, foreground enlargement, machinery and lighting are illustrative. The drive has not been depicted as found or recovered. Visible machinery represents ordinary landfill activity. Its precise model, factory markings and physical condition are not authenticated. The handwritten 2009 tape is an invented prop detail acknowledging the mining year; it is not claimed as an actual historical label. DOCKS WAY / NEWPORT is a geographical clue, not an exact reproduction of the original site sign.

Review v3 is a local edit of v2 with both exact original crypto style references directly supplied. The v1 art is an additional restoration reference. The version-one sign corrections remain documented in the original source package.
