## Historical claims

- Vitalik Buterin, Critical Update re DAO Vulnerability, 17 June 2016: https://blog.ethereum.org/2016/06/17/critical-update-re-dao-vulnerability. Contemporaneous primary source for recursive split calls, the child DAO, at least roughly another 27 days before withdrawal, address 0x304a554a310c7e546dfe434669c62820b7d83490 and the continuous two-word phrase remain calm. The post distinguishes the application vulnerability from Ethereum itself. The post also proposed a soft fork; the book does not claim that proposal was the eventual response.
- SEC Release 81207, 25 July 2017, printed pages 4 and 9: https://www.sec.gov/files/litigation/investreport/34-81207.pdf. Official retrospective account for DAO token purchases, participant project votes, the June 17 attack, the July 20 fork and continuation of the original chain as Ethereum Classic. No financial or legal advice is supplied. The chapter avoids unnecessary precision about amounts.
- Vitalik Buterin, Hard Fork Completed, 20 July 2016: https://blog.ethereum.org/2016/07/20/hard-fork-completed. Contemporary primary source confirming the later hard fork and transfer of DAO funds into a recovery contract. This date is separate from the exploit and the split() label in the art.
