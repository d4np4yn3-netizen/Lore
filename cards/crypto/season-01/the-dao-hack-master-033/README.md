## Story and visual boundary

The four details are the split() function label, nested repeated calls and paper receipts, the locked Ether with ≈27 DAYS, and 0x304a…3490. The latter abbreviates `0x304a554a310c7e546dfe434669c62820b7d83490`, named in the contemporary alert. The front screen and repeated receipts are illustrative metaphors for recursive execution. The temporary withdrawal restriction is approximate, not a countdown to the fork.

The young-Vitalik-inspired character, room and response actions are fictional staging. No documented actions, actual room or attacker identity are claimed. The DAO was an application on Ethereum; this exploit was not a break of Ethereum consensus. The 17 June exploit and contract split() function are distinct from the 20 July hard fork. There is no cross-card cameo prop; 019 supplied a likeness reference and 006 belongs only to earlier composition lineage.

The [17 June alert](https://blog.ethereum.org/2016/06/17/critical-update-re-dao-vulnerability) supports the phrase and exploit details.
