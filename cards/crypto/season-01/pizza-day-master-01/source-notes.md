preferred toppings; that is not proof of the actual pizzas' complete toppings.

on the selected napkin: preserve the approved raster, but do not claim this is
an unbranded mark, a partnership or evidence of permission.

The distant bridge is the intended Jacksonville/Dames Point Easter egg. The
room, window viewpoint, night setting and floating coins are an imagined
composition, not a reconstruction of Laszlo's home or the actual delivery hour.
