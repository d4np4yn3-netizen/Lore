# 015 The First Halving — source and art notes

- [Bitcoin.org halving history](https://bitcoin.org/en/halving): 28 November 2012, height 210,000, subsidy 50 to 25 BTC.
- [Contemporary block discussion](https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=128200.0): the first halving block and Slush's confirmation that his pool found it.
- [Slush's contemporaneous reply](https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=119168.msg1363895): new pool member "laughingbear" and ordinary GPU.
- [Block 210,000 record](https://www.blockchain.com/en/explorer/blocks/btc/210000): 25 BTC subsidy and 13.56295554 BTC fees (38.56295554 BTC total reward).

The observatory, clockwork mechanism, miners sharing a railing and continuing belt are symbolic, not a reconstruction of a physical 2012 meeting. The red-haired miner is a callback to card 011's approved fictional character; the beanie and bear patch allude to the pool account, not a verified portrait. The GPU signifies modest hardware as described by Slush. The fee tray is kept separate from the 25 BTC subsidy. "50 TO 25." is a concise editorial caption, not a sourced quotation.
