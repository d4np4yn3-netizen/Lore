# The Whitepaper — source notes and interpretation

Created 20 September 2026 after Dan said: “Ok, amaze me with Whitepaper”. Dan subsequently approved the exact completed Review-v1 with “Approved, looks pretty cool for a card”. Final art, Legendary treatment and caption are visually approved; approval.json records the full instruction and exact file hashes.

## Date and phrase

The original announcement is dated 31 October 2008:
https://www.metzdowd.com/pipermail/cryptography/2008-October/014810.html
The approved caption NO TRUSTED THIRD PARTY. is a continuous excerpt from its opening sentence; capitalisation and final punctuation are normalised. The announcement subject is Bitcoin P2P e-cash paper. This is the publication event, not the January 2009 Bitcoin network launch.

https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf
The paper has nine pages including references. The title and author identify the hero sheet. Its references include b-money and Hashcash. Both primary sources were inspected during concept preparation on 20 September 2026.

## Four clue groups

- Nine visible sheets: one hero sheet and eight secondary sheets.
- 31 OCT 2008 stamp on the main page, representing the announcement date.
- Original mailing-list subject on the left secondary page.
- b-money and Hashcash labels on the right secondary references page.

Only the short title, author and clue labels are meant to reproduce source text. Other lines, illustrative node diagrams and the date-stamp placement are decorative interpretation, not faithful facsimiles of individual PDF pages. The original document does not depict the imagined city or glowing network.

## Illustration and build

Both exact approved HODL v2 and Birth of Doge v2 artworks were verified, viewed and supplied directly to built-in ImageGen as mandatory whole-scene style inputs. Their subjects and rooms were not composition templates. See reference-sources.json and generation-prompt.txt. The output is a new illustration and its exact bytes are embedded unchanged in the SVG.

The existing Legendary LORE-FRONT-v4 template, original fonts and deterministic renderer are used. Only content fields, illustration and demo QR are populated. No geometry, typography size, badge spacing, logo or footer layout is redesigned. Card output is the existing 900 x 1260 visual format; physical trim remains 63 x 88 mm. Demo QR only; no print or website release.
