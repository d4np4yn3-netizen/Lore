## Represented event and date

The card represents Carlos Matos's convention speech, dated 28 October 2017 in
the event chronology at https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/bitconnect-carlos and
corroborated at https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitconnect . The former cites an
original video titled Carlos From Ny — Amazing Show — Bitconnect Annual Ceremony
28/10/2017. That original linked video was unavailable during research.
This is secondary event-date evidence, not independent original-video verification.
The speech date is distinct from the later lending/exchange shutdown in 2018.
