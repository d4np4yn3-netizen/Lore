# 083 - The Merge

15 SEP 2022 / ETHEREUM / MYTHIC

WEN MERGE?

## The story

On 15 September 2022, Ethereum completed The Merge, joining its existing Mainnet execution layer to the Beacon Chain's proof-of-stake consensus system. The work had been years in preparation. Launched on 1 December 2020, the Beacon Chain had operated alongside Mainnet, establishing its validator network while the original chain continued handling transactions under proof-of-work. Two systems were ready to become one.

The final connection followed a carefully staged sequence. Bellatrix prepared the consensus layer; Paris triggered the execution layer's transition when a predetermined total mining difficulty was reached. Validators staking ETH replaced miners as block producers, while Ethereum's accounts, applications and transaction history remained intact. Picture a bridge receiving its final span: a connection completed while the life it carried continued.

Ending energy-intensive mining reduced Ethereum's energy consumption by an estimated 99.95%. That striking change was the upgrade's clearest immediate environmental result, but its scope mattered. The Merge did not substantially lower gas fees or directly deliver a major increase in transaction throughput; those goals depended on separate scaling work. Its achievement was to change how the network reached agreement without discarding what had already been built, completing a long-planned crossing and laying groundwork for further upgrades.

## Details in the artwork

1. **The final span** A crane lowers the missing bridge section between two established structures. This fictional construction scene symbolises the integration of Mainnet execution and Beacon Chain consensus.
2. **The Beacon lighthouse** The purple beacon recalls card 062, Beacon Chain. Its guiding light connects the consensus chain launched in 2020 with the network transition completed in 2022.
3. **Builders across the collection** The workers and construction imagery echo card 028, The Ether Sale, and card 032, Ethereum Goes Live. They turn separate milestones into one continuing story of building Ethereum.
4. **A city already alive** The Ethereum emblem hangs over a brightly lit city. Buildings, lights and flowing water suggest an existing world continuing through the transition, not a network being created from nothing.
