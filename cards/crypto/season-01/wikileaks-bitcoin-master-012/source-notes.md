# 012 source and artwork notes - review

- Event: WikiLeaks' public 14 June 2011 announcement accepting Bitcoin donations. The original tweet is reproduced as Figure 0.1 in Reid and Harrigan, *An Analysis of Anonymity in the Bitcoin System* (July 2011): https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.4524 . Its words include “WikiLeaks now accepts anonymous Bitcoin donations”; the proposed card line “BITCOIN DONATIONS.” is a continuous excerpt. The published address is intentionally not reproduced.
- Contemporaneous report: Andy Greenberg, *Forbes*, 14 June 2011: https://www.forbes.com/sites/andygreenberg/2011/06/14/wikileaks-asks-for-anonymous-bitcoin-donations/ . It identifies restrictions involving Visa, Mastercard, Bank of America and PayPal. The illustrated closed signs are categories, not documentary replicas.
- Organization's later account: https://wikileaks.org/Banking-Blockade.html . Treat its characterizations of the blockade as its claims, not independent legal findings.
- Privacy boundary: The 2011 Reid/Harrigan paper itself warns that public-address donations may be analyzed and linked; don't say Bitcoin was inherently anonymous or impossible to block.
