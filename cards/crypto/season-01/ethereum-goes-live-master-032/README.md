## Story and visual boundary

The four details are the Frontier sign, genesis zero, initial 5,000 block-gas limit and unfinished Ethereum-shaped landmark. The city and glowing pipes are an imagined metaphor for computational work, not a historical site or central gas supply. The 5,000 figure is gas per block, not a gas price or pressure reading. Card 028, The Ether Sale, provides thematic city continuity; no returning cameo prop is claimed.

The chapter and website share the approved story and source notes. Primary references are [Ethereum Launches](https://blog.ethereum.org/2015/07/30/ethereum-launches), [Frontier is coming](https://blog.ethereum.org/2015/07/22/frontier-is-coming-what-to-expect-and-how-to-prepare) and [Ethereum’s gas guide](https://ethereum.org/developers/docs/gas/).
