# Binance Settles source checks

Checked 24 October 2026. These notes concern the historical 21 November 2023 resolutions, not current legal advice or Binance's present regulatory status.

## Company and individual are separate

The DOJ announcement gives Binance's total as $4,316,126,163, comprising $2,510,650,588 in forfeiture and a $1,805,475,575 criminal fine. This amount is the company resolution, not CZ's personal penalty. DOJ describes the company charges involving Bank Secrecy Act duties, unlicensed money transmission and sanctions violations.

The same announcement records CZ's separate guilty plea for failing to maintain an effective anti-money-laundering program under the Bank Secrecy Act and his resignation as CEO. No later custodial sentence is depicted or assigned to this date.

Source: https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/binance-and-ceo-plead-guilty-federal-charges-4b-resolution

## Coordinated obligations cannot simply be added

DOJ, FinCEN, OFAC and CFTC had distinct roles in the coordinated resolutions. Credits and offsets connect the monetary obligations. The book deliberately avoids adding agency headline figures to the DOJ total. Treasury describes compliance changes and a five-year monitorship. The CFTC agreement announced on 21 November was still subject to judicial approval at that point; the chapter preserves that distinction.
