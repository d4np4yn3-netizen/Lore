# 095 - Six Figures

05 DEC 2024 / BITCOIN / COMMON

NOW THE HERD FOLLOWS.

## The story

The bull from Four Figures returns to the same valley, now broad-shouldered and running at the head of a herd. His white blaze and white foreleg remain unmistakable. Dust rises around the charging animals, and the familiar wooden sign now reads $100,000.

Bitcoin first traded above $100,000 on 5 December 2024 in UTC, during the Asian morning and the previous evening in the United States. Reuters reported a high of $103,649 before the price slipped back below $100,000 later that day. The crossing followed a rally accelerated by Donald Trump's US election victory and expectations of a more crypto-friendly administration.

Earlier that year, the US Securities and Exchange Commission had approved the listing and trading of spot bitcoin exchange-traded product shares. These products opened another route to bitcoin exposure through securities markets. The SEC made clear that its decision was approval of those shares, not an endorsement of bitcoin.

The journey from the first sign to the second had included sharp reversals, with bitcoin falling below $16,000 after FTX collapsed in 2022. By December 2024, it had reached six figures. The bull's familiar markings carry the earlier card into this louder, busier scene: where he once stood alone, a herd now follows through the gate.

## Details in the artwork

1. **Two more digits** The $100,000 sign answers the $1,000 marker on card 018. Its 05 DEC 2024 date fixes the later milestone.
2. **The same bull, grown** The chestnut coat, white forehead blaze and single white foreleg carry the young bull's identity into the larger, stronger lead animal.
3. **The following herd** The open space behind the young bull has filled with charging animals. Their varied coats and horns give the crowd its own character.
4. **The familiar way through** The spreading tree, mountain ridge and wooden gate return from Four Figures. Dust and flying earth replace the stillness of the earlier scene.
