The engineering lab, Charlie Lee's gesture, paired silver/gold machines and airborne blocks are visual storytelling, not a historical photograph. The networks are separate; the image is not evidence of merged mining. The actual card QR covers part of the Whitepaper sheet while the book's full artwork reveals it.
