# Digital Silver — master 014

Approved Uncommon card for the 13 October 2011 Litecoin launch. The selected art is the darker lab composition with silver/gold mining-machine illumination and a Whitepaper-page callback, without a Casascius coin or moon. `art.png` is the full-bleed book artwork. `card-data.json` and the standard print-v1 renderer produce the live-QR card PNG and editable SVG.

The permanent QR resolves to `https://lore-site-v1.vercel.app/crypto/014/`, which redirects to the story page. The two-page A4 book spread is `book/crypto-season-01/proofs/014-litecoin-launches-full-art-spread-v1.pdf`; page one shows the unoccluded art, and page two contains the story and decoded clues. `approval.json` records Dan's publication instruction, and `source-notes.md` separates source-supported facts from stylized imagery.

Digital publication is approved. Physical print and book reproduction release remain pending separate approval; a screen-decoded QR is not a physical scan test.
