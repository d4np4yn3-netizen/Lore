# The Split source and visual record

## Historical evidence checked 4 October 2026

- Vitalik Buterin, [Hard Fork Completed](https://blog.ethereum.org/2016/07/20/hard-fork-completed), 20 July 2016: block 1,920,000 enacted an irregular state change, transferring DAO-related Ether into the WithdrawDAO recovery contract. The post separately discusses the option to follow the non-fork chain.
- [Ethereum protocol timeline, DAO fork](https://ethereum.org/ethereum-forks/#dao-fork): 20 July 2016 fork, recovery purpose, and original-chain continuation as Ethereum Classic.

The 17 June application exploit and the 20 July protocol intervention are distinct events. The fork did not rewrite earlier transaction records; it changed particular balances at one block. ETH adopted the recovery change; ETC continued without it. The art depicts a symbolic disagreement, not a literal railway or identified historical people. The return arrow and padlock are symbolic positions rather than an assertion that either camp was morally superior. The marker is block height, not an Ether amount. No recurring cross-card prop is present.
