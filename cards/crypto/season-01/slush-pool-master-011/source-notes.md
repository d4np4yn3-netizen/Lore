# Slush Pool - 011 source and art notes

Dan approved publication on 27 September 2026: “Okay, I approved upload to GitHub on the site.”. The selected art was generated from the initial three-miner concept and a subsequent edit correcting the right-hand monitor and making the gold Bitcoin block the main light. The artwork, card PNG/SVG and book PDF are exact copies of the reviewed files. Dan chose to leave out the proposed tiny tap and any other crossover item.

Original forum announcement: https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=1976.0. Slush's original text was posted 27 November 2010, includes “Are you interested in?”, the getwork feature, proportional portions of the 50 BTC block reward and the need to trust the pool operator. The November date is the proposal, not the first operational block. Braiins dates launch to 16 December 2010: https://braiins.com/blog/hashrate-as-a-commodity-bitcoin-mining-pools. The cave, pool, golden block, reward trays and anonymous miners are symbols rather than documentary scenes.
