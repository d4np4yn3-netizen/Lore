# Historical and artistic notes

Primary: [Chainlink: Connected Consensus on Ethereum](https://chain.link/blog/chainlink-connected-consensus-on-ethereum), Sergey Nazarov, 30 May 2019.

The launch announcement describes the initial ETH/USD reference feed, three data sources and three independent node operators. Future expansion was planned; reliable inputs still depended on sources and operators. The card does not imply every outside claim becomes true.

The four visible details are the date plaque, ETH/USD screen, three numbered beacons, and Ethereum landmark over a completed city. The graph is illustrative, not a historical price series. People, geography, city and light paths are fictional metaphor, not documentary location or literal architecture. The hexagonal beacon housing is a design motif, not a claim of endorsement or licensing.
