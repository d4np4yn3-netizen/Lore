# 053 - Chainlink Mainnet

30 MAY 2019 / ETHEREUM / COMMON

THE WORLD, ON-CHAIN.

## The story

On 30 May 2019, Chainlink announced its launch on Ethereum mainnet. Smart contracts could execute on-chain, but needed an oracle to bring in information from outside the blockchain.

The initial ETH/USD reference feed used three separate data sources through three independent node operators. Combining multiple inputs aimed to reduce dependence on a single provider.

This was an early network with further expansion planned. Its reliability still depended on the data and operators; the launch was no guarantee that every external claim would become true.

HISTROVE imagines Ethereum as a completed city across a mountain valley. Three blue beams reach it from separate numbered beacons. The landscape turns the distance between external information and on-chain applications into something visible.

A lone traveller watches from the ridge. The city, traveller and light paths are fictional visual metaphors. THE WORLD, ON-CHAIN. expresses the card's editorial theme, rather than quoting the launch announcement.

## Details in the artwork

1. **THE DATE PLAQUE** The first beacon carries 30 MAY 2019, the launch-announcement date.
2. **THE PRICE SCREEN** Its ETH/USD screen identifies the initial reference feed. The rising line is an illustrative graphic, not a historical price chart.
3. **THE THREE BEACONS** I, II and III recall three data sources and three independent node operators. Their blue beams connect directly to the city; they simplify the system rather than diagram its exact architecture.
4. **THE ETHEREUM CITY** An oversized Ethereum symbol rises above the finished city. The back-facing traveller offers a viewpoint into this imagined on-chain world, without representing a documented person.
