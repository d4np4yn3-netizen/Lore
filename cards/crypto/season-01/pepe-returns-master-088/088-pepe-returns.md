# 088 - PEPE Returns

APR-MAY 2023 / PEPE / COMMON

MEME SEASON.

## The story

In April and May 2023, PEPE brought a familiar internet frog into a new meme-token cycle. This was a fungible token on Ethereum, not a revival of the earlier Rare Pepe collectible series. Its enormous stated supply, 420,690,000,000,000 tokens, became part of the project's deliberately meme-heavy identity.

On 5 May 2023, Binance announced that it would list PEPE. The date marks a documented exchange announcement during the token's rapid rise to wider attention, rather than an exact launch date or a guaranteed valuation. The project's own disclaimer says it has no association with Matt Furie, the creator of Pepe the Frog; cultural recognition should not be mistaken for his endorsement.

The oversized inflatable frog turns that attention into a playful image of a meme taking up the whole pool. A supply tag, Ethereum-marked hatch, old Rare Pepes binder and dated newspaper connect the fictional setting to different parts of the story. The relaxed scene is a metaphor, not evidence of a real event, and the older collectibles remain a separate project and asset type.

## Details in the artwork

1. **420.69T supply tag** The tag abbreviates the project's stated 420.69 trillion token supply. It is not a price or market value.
2. **Ethereum hatch** The hatch references the token's Ethereum home. It is visible in the full artwork, beneath the card's lower fade.
3. **RARE PEPES 2016** The binder links back to the earlier Counterparty collectible culture, without equating those assets with the 2023 token.
4. **05 MAY newspaper** The date nods to Binance's 5 May 2023 listing announcement. The newspaper itself is an invented prop.
