# CryptoPunks source verification

Verified 5 October 2026 against all three original book source links. No substantive mismatch found; the approved copy is unchanged.

- [NODE project history](https://nodefoundation.com/cryptopunks): June 2017 launch; creators Matt Hall and John Watkinson; 10,000 unique algorithmically generated portraits; native 24×24 pixels; punk and cyberpunk influence; initial claim free apart from network fee; avatar identity, marketplace and ERC-721 influence.
- [Larva Labs project account](https://www.larvalabs.com/cryptopunks): corroborates launch month, supply, original resolution, free Ethereum-wallet claim, built-in marketplace, profile-picture use and ERC-721 influence. Its archive section identifies the 2017 composite and contract SHA-256 fingerprint.
- [Larva Labs on-chain announcement](https://www.larvalabs.com/writing/2021-8-18-18-0/on-chain-cryptopunks): dated 18 August 2021; explicitly distinguishes the composite-image hash stored in the 2017 contract from the later on-chain images and attributes.

The current overview pages describe the images as permanently on-chain in retrospective present tense. The approved copy correctly uses the dated 2021 technical announcement to distinguish the two stages.

FREE TO CLAIM remains qualified as retrospective NODE wording rather than a verified 2017 launch quotation. The 24 X 24 clue concerns historical original portraits; it does not claim the illustrated reflection has an exact grid. The receipt and street scene are invented symbolic staging. No first-NFT, exact numbered-Punk, rights-clearance, affiliation or endorsement claim is made.

The approved book clue labels are THE 10,000 SIGN, THE CLAIM RECEIPT, THE 24 X 24 NOTE, and THE MATCHING REFLECTION. Title-case Markdown or website labels are presentation normalization only.
