# 052 QuadrigaCX source verification

Verified 9 October 2026 against the official OSC report and EY trustee filing.

- OSC executive summary: January 14, 2019 public death announcement, February 5 creditor protection, over 76,000 clients, Canadian-dollar figures, and the staff findings limitation. https://www.osc.gov.on.ca/quadrigacxreport/
- OSC financial breakdown: approximately C$215m owed, C$46m assets recovered or identified, and C$169m shortfall. It identifies approximately C$115m of internal-platform trading losses and C$28m of external-platform losses. The recovered/identified amount includes later recoveries and is not a January-April 2019 snapshot. https://www.osc.gov.on.ca/quadrigacxreport/where-did-the-funds-go.html
- OSC downfall section: business already in crisis before Cotten died; losing private keys was not the principal explanation. https://www.osc.gov.on.ca/quadrigacxreport/downfall-of-quadriga.html
- EY trustee factum, January 19, 2021, Part I paragraph 3 (PDF page 3): April 11, 2019 termination and bankruptcy assignment order; April 15 assignment into bankruptcy. The chapter uses the unambiguous month-level date April. https://documentcentre.ey.com/api/Document/download?docId=32800&language=EN

The narrative attributes findings to OSC staff. The source report expressly warns that these views were not tested before a court or OSC tribunal. No conclusion of criminal guilt or court judgment is asserted.

All figures remain approximate report values. The C$215M artwork label denotes client liabilities, not the cash in the safe. The archive, papers, claimant and safe are fictional symbolic imagery. Torn papers are not a factual claim of destroyed evidence. The small printed key note, partly hidden among folders below the balances sheet, remains a secondary narrative clue. It is symbolic printed imagery, not a discovered physical or usable key.
