# The Merge — source notes

Primary sources checked during concept preparation:

1. https://ethereum.org/roadmap/merge/
   - The actual Merge date was 15 September 2022.
   - The Beacon Chain launched on 1 December 2020.
   - Ethereum estimates an approximately 99.95% energy-consumption reduction.
   - Its spaceship engine-replacement analogy motivates this original illustration.
2. https://blog.ethereum.org/2022/08/24/mainnet-merge-announcement
   - Bellatrix and Paris are the consensus-layer and execution-layer upgrades.
   - The proposed caption uses the words “wen merge?” from the closing line,
     changed to uppercase to match the card style. This phrase was published
     before the event, not a claimed cockpit transcript or finalization quote.

Bellatrix epoch 144896 and any unverified final block/time are deliberately absent.
All engineers, hardware and the spacecraft are invented; the labels illustrate
verified technical history without presenting the scene as a literal reconstruction.
