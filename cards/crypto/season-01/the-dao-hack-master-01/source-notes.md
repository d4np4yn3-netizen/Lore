engineers are symbolic scenery. They are not a reconstruction of an actual
building or historical participants. The exploit was in The DAO code, not
Ethereum consensus.

The previous large lower purple return pipe has been removed. Violet energy
travels one way from the main vault toward the receiver. A small amber control

separation prevents the loop from suggesting that stolen funds flowed back.
The child vault remains sealed, with a luminous inset window and its lock display.
The engineers are larger and more expressive. These are interpretive art choices,
not additional historical facts or a technical contract-flow diagram.

explicitly abbreviated receiving address 0x304a…3490. The artwork's rendered
