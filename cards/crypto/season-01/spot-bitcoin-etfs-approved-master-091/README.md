## Historical and visual boundaries

On 10 January 2024, SEC Release 34-99306 approved exchange rule changes covering eleven proposed spot Bitcoin exchange-traded products. It was one order, not eleven separate orders. The event concerned listing and trading product shares, not SEC endorsement of Bitcoin. Trading began for the first wave on 11 January; the text does not claim every approved proposal launched that day.

The 1 July 2013 Winklevoss document is a registration filing, not a denial. The disapproval dates shown are 10 March 2017 for Winklevoss, 9 October 2019 for Bitwise and 29 June 2022 for Grayscale. Illustrated document headings are historical references, not legal facsimiles.

The paper mountain is fictional. The Lower Manhattan skyline is grounded in a photograph by Percival Kestreltail from Liberty Island, dated 23 April 2021, licensed CC BY-SA 3.0. See source/geography-reference-attribution.txt for attribution, source and license links. The final scene reinterprets the skyline in anime style; no exact camera position or sunrise astronomy is claimed. This record does not certify commercial rights clearance for the final illustration.

The returning HODL character connects cards 021 and 055. The whisky bottle specifically recalls card 021. FINALLY. is approved editorial copy, not a historical quotation.
