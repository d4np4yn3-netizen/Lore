# Birth of Doge — sources and interpretation

## Date and phrase

https://dogecoin.com/dogepedia/articles/history-of-dogecoin/
The official history gives 6 December 2013 as Dogecoin's launch date and identifies
Billy Markus and Jackson Palmer as its creators. It describes the Kabosu meme
origin. The official site footer credits Jackson Palmer and Shibetoshi Nakamoto.

https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=361813.0
The official announcement thread title contains “very currency”. Early reply
subject lines on 8 December 2013 also preserve it. The opening post was later
edited; its current client version and later protocol details are not launch-day
evidence. VERY CURRENCY. is a continuous excerpt with capitals and punctuation
normalised. It is launch-era wording, not a claim that this thread was posted on
6 December. The card represents the launch, so its date remains 06 DEC 2013.

The user accepted the proposed caption/concept by saying “ok, make it!” after the
proposal. Dan subsequently approved the exact Review-v1 card and authorised GitHub upload on 19 September 2026.
