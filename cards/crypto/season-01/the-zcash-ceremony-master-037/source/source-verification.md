## Historical qualifications

ONE HONEST WITNESS is an approved editorial paraphrase, not a quotation. Under the protocol’s assumptions, one honest, uncompromised participant securely destroying their private contribution was sufficient to prevent reconstruction of the combined secret. This does not remove every trust assumption or exclude software and cryptographic flaws.

The ceremony ran on 22–23 October 2016. Todd’s account dates the later destruction phase to 24 October, and the network launched on 28 October. Snowden’s alias was John Dobbertin; his identity became public in 2022.

## Illustration and likeness

Six stylised likenesses gather around an imagined altar, while the historical participants worked in geographically separate locations. Six crystals and connecting light symbolise the private contributions and shared public result. The altar, floating coin, crystals, gathering and jar are invented staging. The detached CPU-like board is a generic symbolic radio-removal/air-gap cue, not an exact Wi-Fi card. The jar combines Todd’s 24 October hardware-destruction phase with the 22–23 October computation. No physical coin was summoned and no cross-card cameo is claimed.
