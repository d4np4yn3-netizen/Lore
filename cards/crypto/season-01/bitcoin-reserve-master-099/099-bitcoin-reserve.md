# 099 - Bitcoin Reserve

06 MAR 2025 / UNITED STATES / EPIC

UNCLE SAM LEARNS TO HODL

## The story

Uncle Sam stands on the Treasury steps with one hand resting on a monumental Bitcoin. Beneath it, a small plaque states the new position plainly: not for sale. Behind him, Alexander Hamilton's statue watches over a very different kind of reserve asset.

On 6 March 2025, President Donald Trump signed Executive Order 14233, establishing the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve. Treasury was directed to administer the reserve using eligible Bitcoin already in its possession following final forfeiture. Other federal agencies were asked to assess their authority to transfer qualifying holdings.

Bitcoin deposited in the reserve was to be retained rather than sold. The order also created a separate US Digital Asset Stockpile for assets other than Bitcoin, with different management rules. For additional Bitcoin, Treasury and Commerce were directed to develop budget-neutral acquisition strategies that would impose no extra costs on taxpayers. The starting point was existing government holdings, not an automatic market-buying programme.

The card turns that policy into a physical gesture. Uncle Sam steadies the coin instead of offering it to a buyer, while the Treasury and its first Secretary place the scene within the history of public finance. A familiar national character has acquired a new habit: holding on.

## Details in the artwork

1. **Uncle Sam's grip** The star-banded hat and striped trousers identify the national personification. His hand rests firmly on the Bitcoin, turning the reserve's holding policy into a simple gesture.
2. **The Treasury steps** The columned facade recalls the real Treasury building. The setting points to the department charged with administering the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve.
3. **Hamilton keeps watch** The statue evokes Alexander Hamilton, the first Treasury Secretary. His real monument stands on the building's south plaza, linking an older financial institution to the new reserve.
4. **Not for sale** The plaque condenses the order's rule for Bitcoin deposited in the reserve. Its 06 MAR 2025 date marks the signing, five days before publication in the Federal Register.
