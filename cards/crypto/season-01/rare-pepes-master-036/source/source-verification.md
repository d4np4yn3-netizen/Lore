# 036 source verification

The merchant, collector, paper receipt, market exchange, frog footprint and Moroccan souk are fictional staging for digital collecting. The pictured cards are invented designs, not reproductions of the original Nakamoto card. The footprint is not a real authentication method. No Morocco origin or cross-card cameo is claimed. The 300 refers only to the original RAREPEPE issuance, not all Rare Pepe editions or present circulating supply.

Sources: [original issuance record](https://tokenscan.io/tx/563597); [Counterparty asset documentation](https://docs.counterparty.io/docs/basics/assets/counterparty-assets/); Book of Kek's [project history](https://wiki.pepe.wtf/chapter-2-the-rare-pepe-project/the-rare-pepe-blockchain-project) and [Series 1 archive](https://wiki.pepe.wtf/chapter-2-the-rare-pepe-project/the-rare-pepe-blockchain-project/series-and-card-specific-lore/series-1). Architecture was inspired by two user-supplied photographs with no supplied credit metadata. The souk is fictional staging. No cross-card cameo is identified.
