# Next proposed card — Birth of Doge

Assistant recommendation, 19 September 2026. Concept only; not visually approved.
Subject DOGECOIN; proposed Epic; event date 06 DEC 2013.
Proposed title BIRTH OF / DOGE. Proposed phrase VERY CURRENCY.

## Art direction

Kabosu sits with crossed front paws and her recognisable sideways glance on a
second-hand sofa in a wildly improvised home mint. A tiny homemade coin press
has escaped all control: golden doge coins, paper and colourful meme notes spiral
through the room, while the dog remains completely unimpressed. Warm honey-gold
light with electric pink and cyan accents; bold drawn ink and controlled cel
shadows throughout. Strong comedy and character, with the face dominating at
card size. This is an original fantastical scene, not a reconstruction of Kabosu's
home or either founder's workplace. No human founders depicted or implied present.

Use original approved LORE style references and the existing Epic template.
Leave calm header/title areas and keep all clues above the title fade.

## Four proposed Easter eggs

1. NINTONDO on an old game cartridge tucked by the sofa: the exact genesis-block
   timestamp string in Dogecoin's source. The cartridge is an artistic invention.
2. SHIBETOSHI on a small handwritten development notebook: Billy Markus's alias,
   credited in the official Dogecoin site footer.
3. 22556 as a tiny computer port/service label: Dogecoin mainnet's default port
   in the source. This is not a collector number or edition total.
4. 06 DEC 2013 on a little desk calendar: official Dogecoin launch date.

## Primary evidence and date distinctions

- https://dogecoin.com/dogepedia/articles/history-of-dogecoin/
  Confirms launch on 6 December 2013, Billy Markus and Jackson Palmer, and the
  Kabosu meme origin. Footer credits Jackson Palmer & Shibetoshi Nakamoto.
- https://github.com/dogecoin/dogecoin/blob/master/src/chainparams.cpp
  CreateGenesisBlock sets pszTimestamp to Nintondo. CMainParams sets
  nDefaultPort to 22556. These are verified technical clues, not invented numbers.
- https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=361813.0
  Official announcement thread title contains very currency; early reply subject
  lines preserve that wording. The thread is dated 8 December, two days after the
  official launch date. The opening post was later edited; do not use its later
  client version or later consensus details as a facsimile of launch-day software.
  VERY CURRENCY. is a short exact excerpt with capitals and punctuation normalised;
  it is proposed launch-era wording, not asserted to have been posted on 6 December.
- https://kabochan.blog.jp/archives/9733755.html
  Original owner's post associated with the meme photograph. Consult its image as
  factual reference for Kabosu's expression before drawing, alongside the separate
  LORE style references. Do not copy the photograph or blog composition wholesale.
