# Proposed next card — CASH OUT

Status: researched proposal, not selected artwork. Dan authorised moving to the next card after approving Buried Fortune. Recommended rarity Uncommon, date 29 OCT 2013 (public launch). Proposed title CASH / OUT. Proposed editorial caption BITCOIN MEETS CASH. No image generation yet.

## Documented event

The Robocoin kiosk at Waves Coffee House in Vancouver was the first permanent public Bitcoin ATM installation, with its public launch generally dated 29 October 2013. It allowed buying and selling Bitcoin for cash. Earlier conference demonstrations are separate from this permanent installation.

Primary reporting: [CoinDesk launch interview, 30 October 2013](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2013/10/30/robocoin-launches-bitcoin-atm-in-vancouver). The operator was Bitcoiniacs; Robocoin provided the kiosk. Its palm scanner and bidirectional buy/sell operation are documented. This is historical research, not present-day product or compliance guidance.

[WIRED launch photo report, 29 October 2013](https://www.wired.com/2013/10/bitcoin-atm-gallery/) documents an earlier test customer the previous evening. That purchase must not be relabelled as a first-ever cash withdrawal. For the proposed 29 October launch card, depict an illustrative customer using the cash-out function, not an identified historical first withdrawal.

[WIRED installation report, 28 October 2013](https://www.wired.com/2013/10/robocoin-here/) records a temporarily misplaced key on a cafe table. A little cabinet key beside a coffee cup is a sourced visual callback; its placement on launch day would be artistic.
