Visual concept: nine illuminated paper sheets sweep upward in a deliberate arc
over a dark invented city. Lines from the pages become glowing connections
between rooftops, turning an abstract idea into a visible network. One leading
page carries the title; the rest use sparse diagram fragments and drawn marks.
Deep indigo shadows and parchment-gold light, confident anime/manhwa contours,
strong focal silhouette and quieter lower area for the pinned card layout.
This is a creative metaphor, not a reconstruction of Satoshi's location or identity.
