## Art direction

A vast original Ethereum spacecraft above luminous storm clouds, with tiny
maintenance figures and mechanical arms replacing its heavy, ember-lit old
engine with a brilliant blue-white core while it remains in flight. Dramatic
scale, expressive hand-drawn anime mechanics, confident ink and cel shadows.

engine-swap analogy, not a real vehicle or an illustration to copy from its site.
The old engine represents proof-of-work; the new core represents proof-of-stake.
