The review sheets are finished-cut previews, not individual printer files. All individual files include bleed and exclude visible safe/cut guides. Print at 100% size, with no fit-to-page scaling.

## Exact selected printer geometry

- Full bleed: 816 × 1110 at 300 DPI = **69.088 × 93.98 mm**
- Finished cut: 744 × 1038 = **62.992 × 87.884 mm**, or **2.48 × 3.46 in**; nominal 63 × 88 mm
- Trim starts at 36/36 px, giving 3.048 mm bleed on each side
- Safe area: 684 × 981 px at 66/64.5 px
- Front frame clearance inside trim: 3.080 mm on every straight side; back: 3.048 mm
- PDF MediaBox/BleedBox: 195.84 × 266.4 pt; TrimBox: 178.56 × 249.12 pt at 8.64/8.64 pt

## Definition and quality

Every PNG is genuinely rendered at the selected 300-DPI canvas, not relabelled 600 DPI. Original illustrations are retained at their native pixel dimensions; their effective resolution in the card crop ranges **465.68–465.99 PPI**. Per-card native dimensions and effective resolution appear in [manifest.json](manifest.json).

The approved brush wordmark originated as a 2172 × 724 raster. Larger intermediate exports do not create extra detail. The crown geometry remains vector. Card PDFs are mixed vector/raster, not fully vector. No sharpening, AI upscaling, colour conversion, foil separations or new printer profile was applied. The flat RGB colours require printer and sample assessment.

All 39 PNGs and actual 300-DPI PDF renders decode to their unchanged live moment URLs. Every original remote v1.2 PNG is hash-verified and compared pixel-by-pixel: changes are confined to the top-right logo area. All non-logo source elements compare exactly. The shared back tagline is 6.48 pt, enlarged for readable physical samples.

Website cards, book pages, clues and website branding are not changed by this print/brand repository release; existing website print links still target their historical v1.2 files until a separate site update.
