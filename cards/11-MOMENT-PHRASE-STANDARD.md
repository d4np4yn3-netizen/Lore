# Moment phrase standard

**Approved direction: Daniel Payne, 12 September 2026.** Each card's small line beneath the moment title should carry a short phrase from the represented moment. This replaces generic descriptive labels such as `THE COOKING VIDEO` on new completed cards.

## Purpose

The title names the moment; the phrase lets the creator's personality come through. Choose words that add humour, emotion or recognition and work with the artwork. The phrase should make the card feel connected to the actual moment.

## Selection and evidence

- Use a short, authentic spoken line or an exact excerpt from the source post represented by the card. Research the specific moment rather than inventing a generic catchphrase.
- Check wording against the original audio/video or source post, and record the source URL plus timestamp or location in the creator's source notes. Fan recollections and reposts are leads, not primary-source verification.
- Offer a small shortlist and a recommendation; Dan agrees the final wording before it is applied. Do not silently paraphrase a quote, combine separate remarks or change its meaning to make it fit.
- Short continuous excerpts are fine. Uppercase presentation and ordinary punctuation may be normalised without changing the words. Agree profanity, censorship or other editorial changes explicitly.
- If wording cannot be verified, record that uncertainty and agree the treatment with Dan. A user-approved visual draft can carry agreed wording with a recorded verification gap; that gap must not be relabelled as verified or silently carried into print release.

## Fixed layout

Use the existing `context` data field / `moment-context` SVG element. Keep its position, font, size, colour and tracking. It remains one line in the left column, ending before x=620 in the 900 × 1260 reference. Prefer a shorter authentic excerpt if needed; do not shrink type or move the QR to fit it.

The phrase is card-specific editorial content, not a new LORE brand slogan. Brand wording, logos, front/back geometry and approved artwork remain governed by their existing locks. Historical v3 reference cards remain unchanged.
