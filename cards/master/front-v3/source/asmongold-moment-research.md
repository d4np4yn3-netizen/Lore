## Six selected only for this visual proof
### 01 / Common / THE $2 STEAK
In his cooking video, Asmongold shows how he prepares his $2 steak. A familiar everyday ritual becomes the subject of the camera.
Source: [How I Cook My $2 Steak](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hETgbsF5yY8). Channel: Asmongold TV. Upload metadata: 2022-10-02T08:00:32-07:00.
Art direction: a humble home kitchen transformed by dramatic manga perspective. Asmongold in a plain white T-shirt proudly and slightly mischievously presents a small cooked steak on a fork, other hand beside the pan, wry confident grin, one eyebrow lifted. Low close camera looking upward past a steaming black frying pan in the lower foreground. Simple practical stove and kitchen cupboards. Curling cooking steam frames his face in expressive ink shapes, warm light from cooking contrasts charcoal and muted bone surroundings. The steak is modest, the presentation absurdly heroic; affectionate understated humour. No chef hat, no armour, no magical fire.

Asmongold in original simple beginner adventurer clothes with light leather shoulder straps, clearly his high forehead, long loose brown hair and scruffy beard; no grand crown or king regalia. Large three-quarter close face turned toward an enormous luminous faceted crystal suspended in a distant fantasy city. His eyes widen and mouth opens slightly in genuine wonder, one hand extended toward the light. A crowd of small varied ORIGINAL adventurer silhouettes welcomes him below, carved pale stone walkways, bright sky. Dramatic spatial depth. Brilliant bone-white and ice-cyan crystal light with restrained pink/violet spectral edges against dense black ink. Light draws him into an unfamiliar world; openness and discovery rather than combat or omnipotence. Spectacular crystal refractions reserved around the environment; keep human face warm and clearly readable. Not a copy of a specific FF city, character, logo or outfit.
