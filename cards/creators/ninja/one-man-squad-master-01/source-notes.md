# Source evidence

Primary source: Ninja, [32 Kill Solo Squads!! Fortnite Battle Royale Gameplay - Ninja](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jwerp2SNiTQ), published 30 January 2018 in the page's UTC−08:00 timestamp, 31 January UTC. Duration: 17:59.

- The 32-kill solo-squads premise is stated in the original video's title. The artwork does not claim a world record.
- The original [thumbnail](https://img.youtube.com/vi/jwerp2SNiTQ/maxresdefault.jpg), retained as `references/cover-alternate.jpg`, carries the words **PERSONAL RECORD**. The proposed printed phrase adds only terminal punctuation. This is thumbnail copy, not a verified spoken quotation.
- The thumbnail depicts a blue-and-silver knight. Actual gameplay previews show an orange/brown gingerbread outfit with white icing stripes and red/green accents. The card follows gameplay for the costume, rather than using the thumbnail's knight.
- Original [preview frame 1](https://img.youtube.com/vi/jwerp2SNiTQ/hq1.jpg), saved as `references/auto1-alternate.jpg`, shows the gingerbread character, wooden structures and Ninja's contemporary facecam. [Preview frame 3](https://img.youtube.com/vi/jwerp2SNiTQ/hq3.jpg), saved as `references/auto3-alternate.jpg`, provides another costume/build view.
- A storyboard sheet supplied by the original public YouTube player, saved as `references/full-match-sheet-2.jpg`, shows additional gameplay, wooden ramps, green hills, storm colors and the launcher. The 5 × 5 sheet is a sequence of preview frames, not evidence of any spoken line.
- Primary preview images were inspected; the entire match and spoken audio were not independently reviewed. No precise spoken timestamp is asserted.

## Artistic interpretation

The card translates Ninja's likeness into the gingerbread costume visible in gameplay and combines match details into a dramatic illustrated scene. It does not depict a literal screenshot or imply that his later official in-game character existed in this 2018 match. The braced firing stance, arrangement of opponents, explosion illumination and airborne props are compositional choices. The rejected v2 illustration used a vaulting pose and foreshortened boot; these were revised in v3 following Dan's feedback.
