https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g2G8xLSH9OI
The archive is titled “Ninja Pon pon - The First of 2018!!!”. The exact stream
date and the title's first-of-year claim have not been independently established.

Dan supplied the original stream screenshots IMG_4919.jpeg through IMG_4922.jpeg
and the orange/yellow burst-and-stars reference IMG_4926.jpeg (also reattached as
IMG_4926(1).jpeg). They ground the blue Luminosity hoodie, dark trousers, short
brown hair, black headset, basement, pedestal fan, wall equipment and NINJA 16 jersey.

Phrase: THE FIRST OF 2018! Dan supplied this as the spoken wording and approved
the complete card with it. Primary audio and exact timestamp remain independently
unverified. No wording from a later interview has been substituted.

The final selected illustration is preserved byte-for-byte in art.png. Maintain
the hand-drawn anime character and room, red/yellow circular burst with red bars
extending farther inward than the yellow ring, hands behind the border, and
white-outlined stars in front of parts of the figure. Ceiling lights and red
emotes are intentionally absent. Ninja's pose, size and placement are selected.
