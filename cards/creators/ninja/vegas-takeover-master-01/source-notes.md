## Factual basis

Event: Ninja Vegas '18, Esports Arena Las Vegas at Luxor, 21 April 2018.
The welcoming open-arm pose, blue tuxedo with black lapels, open white shirt,
pocket square and short silver/lavender-looking hair are grounded in event
photographs. The real arena's hexagonal panels, angular desks and giant LED wall
inform the setting. This artwork interprets the event: foreground crowd,
viewpoint, abstract screens and heightened gold/cyan light are designed for the
card, not a claim that every detail occupied this position in a single photograph.

## Approved phrase

ONE MILLION PERCENT.

This is a continuous excerpt from the quotation attributed to Ninja in the
official HyperX Arena Las Vegas archive description: “One million percent,
this is the best event I’ve ever been a part of.” Uppercase and terminal
punctuation are editorial normalisations. The wording has been checked in the
published description. The spoken audio and exact timestamp have not been
independently verified. The phrase is not a claim about prize money or viewership.
