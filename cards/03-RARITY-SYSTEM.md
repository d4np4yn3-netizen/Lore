# LORE — Rarity System

## Locked rarity ladder

Every creator collection contains six rarity tiers:

1. **Common**
2. **Uncommon**
3. **Rare**
4. **Epic**
5. **Legendary**
6. **Mythic**

These names are locked unless a later brand-level decision explicitly changes them.

## Core rule

**Rarity changes collectability, scarcity, finish and presentation. It does not make the creator more important, more powerful or more valuable as a person.**

LORE is currently designed as a collectible product first. If gameplay is added later, cosmetic rarity should not create pay-to-win power differences.

## Six different moments

The six rarity cards are not six cosmetic versions of the same pose.

Each rarity represents a **different real creator moment** selected through the approved research and creator-approval process.

This means a collector has a genuine reason to complete all six cards: each card tells a different part of the creator’s story.

## Suggested physical treatment direction

Final manufacturing specifications must be validated with printers and physical prototypes.

### Common
- premium matte or satin finish
- minimal special effects
- strong illustration remains the focus

### Uncommon
- subtle selective foil or light texture
- restrained enhancement

### Rare
- more noticeable patterned or selective foil
- highlight key objects or environmental details

### Epic
- holographic or refractive environmental treatment
- effects should support the moment, not cover the artwork

### Legendary
- premium foil treatment, potentially gold/etched accents
- richer border/material treatment
- may include numbered variants depending on print structure

### Mythic
- most ambitious physical treatment
- creator-specific foil or refractive pattern where possible
- full chase-card presentation
- may be serial numbered in very limited quantities

## Mythic principle

A Mythic must feel extraordinary in the hand.

The goal is not “rainbow foil on everything.” Whenever practical, Mythic treatment should reflect the creator or moment.

Examples:

- gaming creator: glitch/pixel/dungeon motif
- finance creator: metallic chart/currency motif
- music creator: stage-light/audio-wave motif

## Visual progression

The six cards should feel like a coherent set when placed together in the official six-slot display.

Where possible, composition, colour or environmental details may create a subtle visual journey across the six cards, but each card must still work independently.

## Serial numbering

Numbering may be used on selected tiers or special variants.

Examples:

- /250
- /100
- /25
- /10
- 1/1

Exact print populations are a commercial/manufacturing decision and are not locked here.
